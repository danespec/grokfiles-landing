Built by One Person

Built by One Person

Grok Archive Hub is built by one person: a husband, father, brother, son, blue-collar worker, and two-time OEF veteran who came home from war with his eyes open. The archive exists to make public records readable: receipts first, conclusions second, corrections visible, open questions preserved.

Public and protected

What is public vs. member-gated.

Public pages explain the evidence and methodology. Protected member routes require Patreon sign-in and an active entitled membership; if the entitlement cannot be verified, the portal fails closed. Discussion tools remain a later feature.
