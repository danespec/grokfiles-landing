Leon Black and Epstein: Financial Records

Read the payment figures and separate financial evidence without conflating a reported total, an allegation and a judicial finding.

Scope: This is an editorial research guide linking previously published GAH analyses and official source context. Related reading is not independent corroboration. No new primary-source authentication or finding of misconduct is claimed.

Why the $158 million and $170 million figures differ

The SEC-filed Dechert review is a commissioned review, not a court judgment. The March 2025 Senate Finance Committee statement reported a higher total than the earlier review. The GAH payment reconciliation traces the two figures and their source scopes. Do not add them together or treat the difference as proof of a separately established offense. Start with EFTA02730996 and EFTA02731023 for the published source notes.

Distinguish oversight and legal outcomes

The referral-status investigation separates the Senate inquiry from any verified prosecutorial outcome. A referral is not an indictment, and a settlement is not necessarily an admission. Read the relevant findings and agreements in their own procedural posture. A documented payment relationship does not automatically establish the payer’s knowledge of every subsequent use of the funds.

Keep the Black Family Partners transactions separate

The ESWW sale reconstruction, repricing record and source-of-funds gap concern discrete transaction mechanics. Payer, asset holder, investment vehicle, valuation and settlement should not be merged. A separate transaction is not part of the payment total unless a source-level reconciliation establishes that inclusion.

Follow the unresolved questions

Compare the Phaidon tax-model review with the Financial Trust transaction study. The Atlas has distinct chains for the Dechert and Senate inquiry, 2013 Southern Trust transfers and family-office advisory records. These are navigation points for distinct questions, not independent corroboration of every claim about the relationship.
