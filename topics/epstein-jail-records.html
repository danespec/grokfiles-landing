Epstein Jail Records: Logs, Video and Evidence

Use the document classes separately to evaluate the record of Epstein’s detention, death investigation and evidence preservation.

Scope: This is an editorial research guide linking previously published GAH analyses and official source context. Related reading is not independent corroboration. No new primary-source authentication or finding of misconduct is claimed.

What do the official findings establish?

The June 2023 DOJ Inspector General report records serious failures in custody and supervision. The medical examiner ruled the death a suicide, and the FBI found no criminality in how Epstein died. GAH’s final-48-hours evidence chronology distinguishes official findings from source-record gaps. This guide is an entry point by record type, not a second incident timeline or an alternate finding about cause of death.

Logs and observation records

The June 27 lieutenant log (EFTA00033413) provides a pre-detention comparison record; the July 22 log (EFTA00035570) is a later operations record. The EFTA00035574 memorandum and EFTA00039660 status rows are separate records with different evidentiary purposes. A posted rounds notice (EFTA00035206) states an interval, not proof of performance. A handwritten visitor log (EFTA00035119) should not be read as a verified roster without a checked entry.

Video and forensic source boundaries

The CH0080 file-window analysis concerns a camera-file list (EFTA00039416), not a claim to have reconstructed unseen footage. The autopsy exhibit-list analysis distinguishes a pointer from the underlying forensic report (EFTA00039356). An exhibit listing, recording window and medical finding are different evidence types. Their existence does not permit a reader to fill in an unobserved interval.

Preservation and the public statements

The August 12 preservation request (EFTA00035053) and August 14 legal hold (EFTA00035035) document administrative steps, not a complete production inventory. The draft email, issued statement and public-affairs questions are separately indexed in Data Set 8 research. Compare the original source scope with Atlas detention and death and the guards prosecution; do not infer the outcome of a particular record request from the existence of the hold.
