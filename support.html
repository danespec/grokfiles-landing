Support the Archive

Contributions help keep Grok Archive Hub’s public archive, source-search work, and reader-facing evidence pages alive without changing the evidence standard.

Public proof stays public

Support funds the work, not the conclusions.

Public evidence remains publicly accessible. Evidence pages, source maps, receipt indexes, dispatches, search routes, and correction paths stay open. Support does not buy access to hidden proof, favorable coverage, a source removal, a finding, a priority conclusion, or editorial treatment.

What support helps cover

Hosting

Document hosting and bandwidth

Keeping source-linked routes, static pages, public assets, and archive-reader paths available.

Processing

OCR and archive processing

Turning hard-to-read files into searchable text while preserving caveats, uncertainty, and source context.

Preservation

Source preservation

Backing up release artifacts, receipt ledgers, source maps, hashes, and correction history.

Search

Search infrastructure

Maintaining the public search path so readers can inspect records directly.

Evidence pages

Evidence-page development

Building reader guides, methodology pages, receipt indexes, and public-safe context layers.

Support options

Use Patreon for the membership bridge.

Patreon is the current creator-support bridge for readers who want to support the archive from outside the site. The existing PayPal and Venmo rails remain available on intended support surfaces, but the public archive does not collect card, bank, password, login, or account information.
