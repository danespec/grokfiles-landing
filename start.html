The five-part reading rule

01 · Claim

What is actually being asserted?

Keep the claim narrow enough that a source can support or contradict it.

02 · Source

What record supports it?

Open the original document, inspect nearby context, and preserve the identifier.

03 · Bias

Who made the record—and why?

Official records, emails, litigation filings, news reports, and OCR each carry different limits.

04 · Silence

What does the record not establish?

A record can show a reference or date while remaining silent about purpose, knowledge, or conduct.

05 · Confidence

How strong is the conclusion?

Confirmed source facts, reasonable inferences, and open questions should never be blended.
