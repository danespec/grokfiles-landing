# GAH — EFTA source-record indexing expansion (November 20, 2026)

Owner direction: Make the actual EFTA source-record archive discoverable, beyond the 87 wiki editorial pages, without violating protected-identity, redaction, or source authenticity limits.

## Initial evidence
- Previously live wiki research sitemap: 87 routes.
- Hidden legacy source-sitemap generator: 49,900 EFTA document URLs.
- All canonical wiki EFTA routes redirected to main-domain thin source locators.
- Backend EFTA source pages carried HTTP and HTML noindex.
- NAS source volume: 592,658 direct OCR files (not all independently screened).

## Indexed-source admission
An offline scan considered 49,900 sitemap EFTA identifiers and 902 additional banking-record source identifiers: 50,802 distinct candidates. The scanner admitted 12,106 records. Removing six duplicate main-site dossier owners produced exactly **12,100 approved EFTA URLs** for this release.

Requirements: an actual resolvable source and OCR excerpt; 800+ normalized display characters and 300+ letters; no obvious personal email, phone, SSN/account identifiers, street addresses, victim/minor abuse cues or protected-identity indicators within the bounded excerpt; any existing banking noindex or manual-review ruling overrides admission. The filter is automated and is NOT a guarantee that every document has received individual privacy or substantive review.

## Routing and search discovery
- Only the 12,100 approved identifiers reach their wiki source pages and receive index,follow in both HTTP response and HTML meta.
- Unapproved source URLs still redirect to the main-site source locator. Previously published main-site EFTA dossiers retain their sole canonical home.
- New EFTA sitemap contains exactly 12,100 self-canonical, real EFTA documents. A sitemap index includes the existing 87-route sitemap and the EFTA sitemap; future partitions have a 30,000-URL ceiling.
- Robots advertises the sitemap index. A legacy unscreened 49,900-URL sitemap alias now redirects to the approved index.
- API auth, source privacy restrictions, redactions, the analytics instrumentation, billing and AdSense policy are not changed. Automatic EFTA documents remain advertising-excluded.
- The wiki research directory adds an EFTA-source collection entry.

## Validation requirements
- Isolated wiki NAS stage served the 12,100-document EFTA sitemap and the original 87-route research sitemap successfully.
- A distributed selection of 120 EFTA documents passed HTTP 200, HTML and response robots index,follow, and exact self-canonical checks; 0 failures.
- Known non-eligible document and main-site duplicate EFTA URLs still redirect.
- Protected wiki research guides retain noindex and security gates remain unchanged.

Sitemap submission is a discovery action, not proof of search engine indexing or rank. Google documentation: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls .
