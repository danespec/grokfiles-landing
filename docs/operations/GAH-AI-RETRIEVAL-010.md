## Corpus publication lifecycle
scripts/build_research_corpus_010.py inventories indexable, canonical public editorial HTML in the checked-out publication. It finds 87 candidates in this baseline, including 17 inherited approved publications and 70 pending privacy review. Pending material is not indexed. An exclusive-create mode-0600 review inventory must be outside the checkout. New approvals require a reviewer/date, exact publication SHA256, approved=true, privacy_reviewed=true and public_editorial=true. This is a review record, not cryptographic proof of reviewer identity. Do not fabricate independent human review.

The default artifact contains the 17 approved publications only, unchanged original titles/descriptions and the existing bounded passage. Approved source changes fail the build rather than silently retaining stale indexing. Re-review and refresh the inherited publication input before rebuilding changed sources. Full-corpus activation remains gated on the 70 publication privacy reviews and independently judged relevance. Local review candidates never become public routes or inference input automatically.

For an expanded approved corpus, the optional pinned local NumPy/scikit-learn toolchain fits TF-IDF/LSA with a fixed seed; no inference API is called. Neural embedding and edge-service comparisons are experiments, not deployment selections. Never treat LSA as neural embeddings or automatically provision Vectorize/Workers AI. Existing source protections and public government-record access remain intact.

## Benchmark and release gates
The frozen fixture has 250 synthetic research queries in 50 five-query families: 150 development, 100 held-out. Family partitioning prevents direct family overlap; it is not independent human labeling. Freeze precedes evaluation. Do not tune quality against the held-out cases. scripts/benchmark_research_010.mjs records actual local operations, cache behavior and candidate outputs for lexical, weighted, RRF and multistage runs. Local wall time is neither Worker CPU nor an end-to-end SLA.

scripts/score_research_010.py binds reviews to the exact fixture, experiment and ordered results and fails closed while judgments are missing. It reports MRR, Recall@5/@10, Precision@3/@5, nDCG@5/@10, judged citation validity, retrieval abstention and applicable subject precision only after every case is independently reviewed. Applicable-question definitions must be set by reviewers, not selected to inflate scores. No human performance target is certified by CI. URL availability, source hashes and claim-level entailment are separate checks.

Run source/runtime build checks, new provenance/privacy/cache/disable controls, existing archive/Atlas/security/analytics tests, independent CI and authenticated staging on both profiles. Verify real 429/reset and isolated bindings, original 009 top results and full archive controls, feed/graph validators, public catalog cardinality, Markdown/llms/robots and training policy. Exclude all harness/review/model-download files from production assets. Promote only the identical validated source tree/artifact; repeat the original 100-search production benchmark, record Pages CPU, errors/retries and bounded NAS observations. No sustained 10-million-request capacity claim follows from these tests; that target is about 3.86 requests/second averaged over 30 days and requires peak, entitlement and origin studies.

## Measurement interpretation and rollback
The November 13 audit estimated 215,987 public AI-classified external requests over 2026-10-13T16:53:00Z to 2026-11-13T16:51:38Z. It used adaptive sampling and requestSource=eyeball with exact public hosts. These are not independently verified operators, unique users, citations or customers. Compare matching windows and dimensions, disclose missing fields and sampling, and do not annualize a short post-release interval into adoption claims. Private Intel, monitoring and internal Worker subrequests are separate scopes. New services do not guarantee AI adoption.

No infrastructure purchase is authorized. Cost scenarios must distinguish included allowances, requests, CPU, limiter requests/duration/storage and optional embedding/vector costs. Whole-host disk/CPU counters include background traffic, RAID and cache effects, not attributable per-query NAS reads. Neural model data is public-only and local; no private research query is sent to a model provider. Actual billed incremental cost and sustainable peak throughput remain unproven until measured.

Rollback current production to 6e3b2d18-d859-4f87-a27a-62c6527a652d if a material regression appears, unless a newer healthy baseline is recorded before promotion. Confirm the canonical deployment and repeat archive/source/security controls. The experimental profile may be disabled independently; do not remove Access, bind production data into staging, restore unsafe old preview bindings, or roll back healthy production merely to exercise a procedure.
