## Finding 1B — a separately documented $150,000 consulting arrangement and a 2026 independent review

Contemporaneous reporting in May 2023 describes **Leon Botstein** receiving **$150,000** during 2016 from Epstein-associated **Gratitude America** under a consulting/advisory arrangement. Released 2016 bank check images have been reported as quarterly payments, including an August 1 check described as the third of four **$37,500** quarterly installments. **These original check images were not independently downloaded in this pass**; do not represent them as GAH-authenticated bank postings. This known arrangement could explain part of the charity's $290,000 consulting-fee return line, but **the available tax form does not name the recipient(s) and does not prove that particular accounting classification**.

Botstein has said that he contributed these funds to Bard College as part of a larger personal gift. However, a **WilmerHale review**, summarized to Bard's trustees **April 30, 2026** and linked by the College in its **May 1 public statement**, said the consulting arrangement was not disclosed to the board at the time, and that because the claimed onward contribution had been merged with Botstein and/or his wife's other contributions over the years, the available documents **could not confirm** that these fees reached Bard. The summary also found nothing illegal in Botstein's dealings with Epstein while criticizing his lack of transparency. **Unable to confirm** does not mean the money was proven not donated. The full investigative review has not been established as publicly released; the cited memorandum is a **summary**, not the full underlying file.

## Finding 2 — bank-account review and discontinued account opening appear in a SIGNED regulatory order

**New York State Department of Financial Services / Deutsche Bank consent order, July 6, 2020**, paragraph **44**, states:

- On **January 4, 2016**, an accountant for Epstein sought a **brokerage account for Gratitude America** at Deutsche Bank.
- The request reached the bank's Americas Reputational Risk Committee, and an external Epstein due-diligence report was required.
- The accountant subsequently reported that Epstein had resigned from the charity and **withdrew the application**. The external report **was not run**.

**Legal status:** This is an affirmative finding recited in a regulator–bank consent order, not an adjudication that Gratitude America's donations were illegal. The document uses anonymized **ACCOUNTANT-1**; it does NOT establish that person was any specific named accountant.

**Separate, corroborative DOJ-released Deutsche Bank email:** **EFTA01361509** (one page; Bates **DB-SDNY-0050691**, **SDNY_GM_00196875**) has subject “Epstein account opening” and says Deutsche Bank's reputational-risk team sought an external due-diligence report and instructed staff to describe the delay to the client as normal internal due diligence. That is documentary evidence about bank internal communications, not proof that every employee complied or that Epstein knew the bank's concern.

## Finding 3 — bank-schedule versus 2016 grant recipients

The same 2016 return reports nine charitable grant recipients totaling **$580,000**, including **$110,000 to Verse Video Education**, which was separately incorporated from Harvard. The **EFTA00027019** Deutsche Bank-derived exhibit lists direct money-market account outflows of **$100,000** to the Bruce and Marsha Moskowitz Foundation (two $50K entries) and **$225,000** to the Melanoma Research Alliance Foundation, corresponding to amounts reported in the tax schedule.

This is stronger evidence of those two money-market-origin payments, but it does NOT show all the charity's accounts or provide direct proof that the entire $580,000 was paid from that account. The remaining recipient payments would need **Gratitude America checking account**, Puerto Rico FirstBank and/or investment account records. No source at hand directly joins Verse Video's $110,000 payment to the sole BV70 deposit through specific commingled cash flows.

## Finding 4 — EFTA redaction-variant claim is an OPEN TEST, not a verified deletion

- **EFTA01345290** — described by third party as exposing those values.
- Both are described as TY2016 Form 990-PF records with **DLN 93491135015217**.

**Verification performed:** The independently accessible **2016 public Form 990-PF tax scan** visibly includes monetary figures and a $290,000 consulting-fees schedule. DOJ official EFTA PDF URLs responded with **age-verification HTML rather than PDF bytes** from the authorized Mac, and alternate image endpoints failed; therefore **no original-image pixel comparison or DOJ-byte SHA comparison was possible here**.

**Publication status:** UNVERIFIED CLAIM. Do not say DOJ “changed redactions”, “silenced amounts”, “removed information”, or “hid donors” based on OCR differences alone. Need original PDFs for BOTH EFTA IDs, confirm page count/identical DLN, and perform a side-by-side image comparison logging differences, dates and distinct disclosed values.
