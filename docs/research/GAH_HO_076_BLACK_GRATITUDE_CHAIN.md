## Material finding — a documented bank-derived transaction

The nine-page document **EFTA00027019 (DOJ Dataset 8)** is a **bank-derived transaction schedule assembled from Deutsche Bank records**. Its Exhibits A and E both list **October 14, 2015: BV70 LLC → Gratitude America Ltd. → $10,000,000**, keyed to **DB-SDNY-0004999**. The exhibit text says that this deposit primarily capitalized Gratitude America's money-market account. It separately cites *Bloomberg* reporting that Black controlled BV70. The exhibit is **not itself the unabridged underlying bank statement**, which has not been independently reviewed here.

**Independent corroboration:** Dechert LLP's January 22, 2021 counsel-prepared review, publicly filed as Apollo **SEC Exhibit 99.1**, expressly reports that Leon Black made a **$10 million donation to Gratitude America in October 2015**. Dechert describes the charity as Epstein-affiliated. This corroborates the identity and amount from a separate investigative review, though it is **not an SEC agency finding or IRS ruling**.

**Tax-report reconciliation:** The **2015 Gratitude America Form 990-PF**, EIN **66-0789697**, as indexed by IRS-derived ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, reports **$10,003,000 in total contributions**, **$10,007,332 total revenue**, **$10,006,564 end-of-year net assets**, and no grants listed as made that year. A separately published transcription of the donor's Schedule B identifies BV70 as its $10 million contributor. There is a **$3,000 difference between the total contribution line and that named gift**; this does not prove missing funds, misreporting, another identifiable donor, or a second bank receipt. Original Form 990-PF page images and item-level reconciliation remain requested.

## The overlooked $5 million: a letter, not a verified additional transfer

The **same Dechert SEC exhibit, footnote 4**, says that investigators found an **August 21, 2015 Gratitude America letter thanking Black for $5 million**, as well as an Epstein-to-Black email acknowledging it. But Dechert explicitly says the investigation **found neither a Black/Family Office document nor bank record substantiating that apparent payment**.

**Release gate:** Do **not** add the letter's $5 million to the documented October $10 million as though $15 million were confirmed. The letter may reflect a pledge, an expectation, an error, a different transfer, or something else; no choice among those explanations is established by the evidence in hand.

## An April 15 claim and the six-month date mismatch

An Epstein email to accountant **Melanie Spinella**, dated **April 15, 2015**, is reproduced on the genuine **HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_023226** page and elsewhere in DOJ EFTA correspondence. It claims $10 million had been paid "today" to Gratitude America while discussing broader compensation, tax-savings estimates and credit calculations. The bank-derived schedule and Dechert review place an actually documented $10 million gift **on October 14, 2015**, roughly six months later.

**Do not collapse the dates.** April is an Epstein assertion in a later-forwarded message; October is separately supported by bank-derived records and the SEC-filed review. This does **not** prove two $10 million transfers or prove that the April assertion was false—the April payment has not been independently traced.

## The following-year Verse Video gift is a distinct evidentiary step

Gratitude America's **2016 Form 990-PF**, as indexed from IRS data, reports **$134,178 total annual revenue** and approximately **$875,935 total expenses**. Its *reported grantee schedule* lists **Verse Video Education, $110,000**. Separate February–May 2016 messages **EFTA00827905** describe the planned grant, IRS exempt status and permission to deposit a Gratitude America check. Harvard's May 2020 investigation expressly excludes this Verse Video gift from its direct-to-Harvard donations review because the recipient was separately incorporated.

The **2016 reported grant** is documented. **Its exact settlement and bank-account funding path are not**: the published EFTA00027019 Exhibit E is a schedule for **one Gratitude America money-market account**, not all its accounts or every downstream charity check. Recipient bank credit, a check image and the grant agreement are still outstanding. Do **not** state that the identical $10 million donor dollars were traced to the $110,000 grant.

Another source, **HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_019205**, describes a "100K" contribution as made **"last year"**. That phrase creates a prior-year possibility, not evidence that the $110,000 2016 gift and the "100K" statement describe one transfer or an unexplained shortfall.

## Separate legal track: House contempt finding on September 16, 2026

**September 15:** The House Oversight Committee voted to advance a contempt recommendation against Leon Black concerning demanded testimony and nondisclosure agreements. **September 16:** The **full House approved a contempt resolution by unanimous consent**, as announced by the committee chair. This is a new congressional procedural status compared with the committee vote; it is **not a criminal conviction, court judgment on Epstein-related wrongdoing, or evidence that any NDA concealed an offense**. The full-house action can be referred for DOJ prosecution, which is a separate step. No specific post-referral charge or disposition has been confirmed in this investigation.
