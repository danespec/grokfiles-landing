## Priority A — an identifiable October 2017 aircraft financial model

The October 23, 2017 message **HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_026764**, from **Barry J. Cohen** to **Jeffrey Epstein** with **Joseph Vinciguerra** copied, has subject **“G650 profit model”** and names an attachment, **Odysseus Profit Model.xlsx**. The message states Vinciguerra reviewed the model with aviation and aviation-tax counsel **Ruth Wimer** and **Dave Deyoe** at MWE.

An October 2 chain in **HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_026216 / 026221 / 026224 / 026455** discusses possibly changing the aircraft manager from Jet Aviation to Executive Jet Management, business profitability, passive-versus-accelerated depreciation and the possible effects of US and foreign travel. In **026221** Cohen says assumed Apollo-related charter payments influenced the expected profit and depreciation model. Epstein repeatedly requested supporting business calculations; one October 3 forwarding message questions the profit/tax results.

**Evidence level:** Original email pages and attachment label authenticated to the extent possible from publicly released *images*. The actual spreadsheet's binary contents, metadata, formulas, inputs, revised versions and results were **not supplied in the OCR collection** (one occurrence of the filename, as an email attachment label). **No charter payment, executed EJM contract, IRS acceptance, profit or depreciation deduction is shown as completed.**

**Next receipt:** Recover the native Odysseus workbook, final signed management and charter contracts, G650 legal owner, Apollo invoices and payment records, aircraft-use log and tax filings. Do not conflate this **2017** aircraft-manager conversation with the separate **2005** police/Jet Aviation records.

## Priority B — Poetry in America: two distinct funding relationships

**HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_028952** includes **December 2, 2015** communications from Harvard professor **Elisa (“Lisa”) New** thanking Epstein for helping with a **Leon Black gift** that, according to New, paid salaries and led to Harvard offering resources including studio space. The correspondence is a recipient's contemporaneous account of the gift's significance. It **does not disclose the original Black gift agreement, its exact sum, sending account or Harvard internal approvals**.

A separate **February–May 2016** exchange, identified as **EFTA00827905**, describes a proposed **$110,000 Gratitude America check** to independent nonprofit **Verse Video Education**. Correspondence asks that the check not be deposited until receipt of IRS nonprofit recognition; a later message seeks Epstein's approval for the recipient to cash it. **Gratitude America's 2016 tax return reports a $110,000 grant** to Verse Video; **Harvard's May 2020 investigation (footnote 6)** explicitly says that this gift was *not made to Harvard* and was excluded from its direct-gift review. Donor tax-return reporting is stronger evidence than a mere pledge, but **original clearing records have not been inspected**.

A **December 2016 year-end letter** attributed to New (HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_019205) calls the money **“100K”**, while donor-side reporting identifies **$110,000**. That may be approximate shorthand or a different accounting convention; it does **not establish a missing $10,000**. Keep as an accounting and denomination reconciliation question.

**Critical separation:** the **2015 Leon Black gift** (specific amount and vehicle not independently established here) is **not automatically the same money** as the **2016 Gratitude America $110,000 grant**. Nor does Harvard provision of space imply the latter grant was a Harvard donation. These documents do not establish complicity in Epstein's crimes by New, Harvard, Black or the nonprofit.

## Priority C — January 2015 Epstein–Maxwell response-planning messages

Original House scans **HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_030600** and **030295** show Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell and advisers discussing whether and how Maxwell should respond to allegations in January 2015. **030295** includes Philip Barden's recommendation for an affirmative public denial for reputational and legal reasons; **030600** includes Epstein's suggestion linking a proposed response to a Clinton-related assertion. **025873** contains further participant claims concerning travel.

**What this establishes:** the participants discussed public relations/legal response tactics. **What it does not:** whether any allegation was accurate, whether a specific person traveled or engaged in wrongdoing, that a statement was knowingly false, or that a final version was published. Source wording such as “disproved” is the correspondent's allegation, not GAH's finding.
