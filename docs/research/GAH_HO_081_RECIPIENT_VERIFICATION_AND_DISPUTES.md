## Outcome 2 — Kuhn Foundation: $650,000 reported, project account and donor influence documented

The Gratitude America 2017 and 2018 grant schedules report **$150,000** and **$500,000** to the **Kuhn Foundation**, respectively. [The Kuhn Foundation's IRS-indexed Form 990-PF records, EIN 33-0783933](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/330783933), show **$1,216,825 in total 2018 contributions from all donors**, not proof that the entire sum originated with Epstein.

In an **August 1, 2017** contemporaneous released email, Robert Lawrence Kuhn says he had opened a **dedicated sub-account** under his foundation for the proposed *Closer To Truth — Radical Breakthroughs* project, seeking to monitor use of Epstein-backed funds. The attached instructions contain sensitive banking identifiers; **do not republish those**. Source: [EFTA00666589](https://yirah.fi/epstein/dokumentti/EFTA00666589) and [EFTA01039342](https://yirah.fi/epstein/dokumentti/EFTA01039342).

A **June 15, 2018** email instructs Kahn to **send $500,000**, following a prior $600,000 discussion; the amount agrees with the donor's annual grant report. A dedicated account and outgoing transfer instruction are **not a complete recipient bank statement or vendor ledger**. [Documentary reconstruction with source EFTA citations](https://epstein-data.com/wiki/people/robert-kuhn).

[CNBC's December 12, 2019 direct interview of Kuhn](https://www.cnbc.com/2019/12/12/jeffrey-epstein-funded-science-tv-episodes-a-year-before-he-arrest.html) reported that Epstein had promised **$1.4 million total**, that actual 2017–18 grants amounted to **$650,000**, and that **as of December 2019 the proposed episodes were unfinished**. Kuhn said he had been unaware of the allegations when introduced to Epstein. These are his statements, not independent confirmation of spending or the production outcome **as of October 2026**.

This is a promising *documented project-purpose* join, unlike a generic “general charitable purposes” line in a donor's Form 990-PF; it is **not proof every donated dollar was spent exclusively on this program**. Obtain the dedicated sub-account's actual receipts, project vendor invoices, episode budget versions, year-end restricted/unrestricted classifications and any fund return or later completion.

## Outcome 3 — two previously denied recipient grants remain unresolved

**New York Concert Artists Association:** Gratitude America's **2016 Form 990-PF** reports **$50,000** to the organization. In **2019**, spokesperson Jennifer Park told The Daily Beast the group **did not receive any such Gratitude America donation**, including after examining the grant schedule. No corresponding clearing check or recipient-account deposit is established by this pass. These are **conflicting donor and recipient accounts**, not proof of diversion or a false return.

**Cancer Research Wellness Institute:** Gratitude America's **2017 Form 990-PF** reports **$75,000**. Its representative Howard Straus told The Daily Beast in 2019 the group had **not received a grant of that magnitude**. Separately the **EFTA00080260 bank-derived MMDA Exhibit E** lists **$25,000 outgoing transfers to a named Cancer Research Wellness Institute on October 2, 2017 and February 28, 2018**. Those two transactions **do not equal an independently confirmed $75,000 deposit in 2017**, and a denial of *that magnitude* does not by itself refute that **smaller entries were listed**. Do not label either $25K as received without destination-account confirmation or endorsement. [Exhibit E full PDF](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/EFTA00080260.pdf).

**Recipient interviews and opposing statements:** [The Daily Beast April 16, 2019 original report](https://www.thedailybeast.com/jeffrey-epstein-has-a-secret-charity-heres-who-it-gave-money-to/); reporting is original interviews but **not a court finding or a bank audit**.

The difference between “reported paid,” “a bank-derived outgoing transaction,” “recipient acknowledges,” “recipient denies,” and “fully cleared/settled” must be preserved in every GAH ledger field. The correct next records are the donor-side check register and the recipient-side bank credit (or returned/cancelled check, rejected transfer notice or duplicate-entity name investigation).
