## Finding

The available record independently distinguishes: (1) pre-CGI development and 2004 Publicis Events correspondence, (2) a $25,000 C.O.U.Q. grant to the William J. Clinton Foundation in 2006, (3) the 2013 TerraMar CGI commitment and recognition, and (4) the Foundation's 2016–17 financial decline and CGI annual-meeting interruption. **No retrieved evidence proves that the 2004 proposed $1 million wire cleared, that Epstein funded it, or that Epstein links caused the CGI restructuring or the Foundation's revenue decline.**

### Organizational scopes — do not combine

- Clinton Foundation operating nonprofit **EIN 31-1580204**. Its IRS Form 990 revenue is not the face amount of CGI member commitments.
- CGI ran inside the Foundation in 2005–09, as a **separately affiliated nonprofit EIN 27-1551550 in 2010–13**, and returned to initiative status in 2013. [Organization/tax counsel chronology](https://www.egclegal.com/annual-financial-reports/). Obtain underlying registration/990 records before definitive corporate control claims.
- Separate Clinton **Family** Foundation **EIN 30-0048438** is not the operating Foundation.
- TerraMar Project Inc **EIN 45-5091884** and C.O.U.Q. Foundation are distinct third-party entities. CGI's [2006 report](https://www.clintonfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/ar_2006.pdf) says member projects are partner commitments, not CGI grants or donations routed through CGI.
