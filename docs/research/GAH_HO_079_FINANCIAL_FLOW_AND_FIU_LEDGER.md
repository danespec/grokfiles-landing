## Major findings and source grade

**Primary financial exhibit:** Original 9-page released bank-derived transaction schedule EFTA00080260, Exhibits A and E, contains an October 14, 2015 $10,000,000 BV70 LLC credit to Gratitude America, followed by dated cash movements from ONE Deutsche Bank money-market deposit account (MMDA). It is a transaction schedule, **not all of the bank's original monthly statements or the charity's complete ledger**. Retrieved RollCall PDF and a second source mirror have identical SHA-256: 0286422265c4305e8d979823b9601f58e4aecb24806879ec5a9ad05ae1c143b2. Identical mirror bytes **do not prove** byte identity with DOJ servers.

**Breakthrough 1 — same-entity transfers, not $8.45M of donations.** In 2016 the exhibit records:
- $7,500,000 from its MMDA to an account at Morgan Stanley / Citibank held **by Gratitude America itself** ($5M on Mar 2, $2M Jul 14, $500K Jul 19).
- $650,000 to its own checking account (Feb 3 $150K; Mar 8 two $250K transactions).
- $300,000 to its own FirstBank Puerto Rico account (Jan 21 $50K, Apr 6 $250K).
- $325,000 directly to *outside* charities: Moskowitz Foundation $100K across Jan/Jul and Melanoma Research Alliance $225K on Jan 29.
- **Total 2016 listed MMDA outflows: $8,775,000; of that, $8,450,000 moved to other Gratitude America accounts, not outside the charity.** The bank exhibit's footnote 10 expressly corrects the intermediary label “Morgan Stanley” to say the receiving account belonged to the charity.

**Breakthrough 2 — 2016 broker trade email supplies confirmation of the investment channel.** Released correspondence EFTA00643676, dated March 2, contains advisor Andrew Atlas's report of three securities purchases for Gratitude America after a $5,000,000 wire was sent by Richard Kahn: 800 LNKD at $119.1347, 7,500 BAC at $13.3725, 3,200 EDIT at $31.8652. Their mathematically computed *reported-order notional* is **$297,570.15**, not evidence of the other $4.7M's disposition. A later March 8 EFTA00704168 discussion of EDIT appreciation explicitly refers to **unrealized** gains, not realized profits. Need actual Morgan monthly statements and final trade confirmations.

**2017 category audit:** Exhibit E lists $1.3M from MMDA to the charity's own checking/FirstBank accounts, $75K directly to outside recipients, plus $2.5M moving in the **reverse** direction from checking back to MMDA. This last $2.5M is neither new charity revenue nor outside spending.
**2018 category audit:** Direct outside transfers in Exhibit E total **$53,493** (Cancer Research Wellness Institute $25K, two Lithuanian organizations $18,493 and $10K); another $2M moved to the charity's own checking.
**2019:** One $314,876 MMDA-to-own-checking transfer.

## Breakthrough 3 — French TRACFIN inquiry and three identified SARs

**Source grade upgraded to ORIGINAL PDF IMAGE INSPECTED:** 3-page EFTA00153569–571, publicly released FBI/FinCEN correspondence, Jan 17–Mar 6, 2020.

- Jan 17, 2020 FinCEN email describes a French FIU (TRACFIN) Egmont request concerning a **€50,000 transfer from Gratitude America's Deutsche Bank account** to an account of **Association pour la Promotion de la Politique Culturelle**. The message characterizes a concern about unclear purpose and possible illicit origin; those were **suspicions communicated in an intelligence request, not proved criminal facts**.
- On Mar 6 a FinCEN detailee says the agency found **three suspicious activity reports associated with Gratitude America**, and asks the FBI about sharing an Egmont response and spreadsheet attached to one SAR. The FBI reply on coordination does not constitute a judicial finding.
- Separately, the publicly filed **2018** Gratitude America 990-PF grants schedule reports **$57,897** to a similarly named Paris association. **Do not call the €50K and $57,897 the same transfer** without original bank debit/credit, wire date, exchange conversion and matching beneficiary identifier. Currency amounts, tax years and matching names alone are not transaction-level proof.
- The underlying three SARs, French request, response spreadsheet and investigative outcome have **not** been obtained or verified and may be protected/non-public.

## Breakthrough 4 — IRS forensic account review in FBI investigative case

**Source grade upgraded to ORIGINAL SINGLE-PAGE FBI IMPORT FORM INSPECTED:** EFTA00128841, dated April 26, 2021, case ID 272B-SJ-3262541, case caption **Southern Trust; Southern Country International; Wire Fraud**. The report title states **IRS FoA analysis of GRATITUDE AMERICA LTD accounts** and the synopsis states an IRS **contract forensic accountant conducted a review of the charity's bank accounts**.

This authentic source establishes an IRS account review was furnished to an FBI case file. It **does NOT disclose the forensic accountant's report, any accusation against a payee, case outcome or criminal indictment**. The listed enclosure is partially redacted and absent from this single-page release.
