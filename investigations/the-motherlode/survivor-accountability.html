Survivor & Institutional Accountability: GROK 095–098

After the telecom campaign closed, the investigation moved into a deliberately stricter source-and-identity regime.

Grok Archive Hub Editorial Desk · October 1, 2026

Workspace correction. The current artifact tree supports this phase through GROK 098, not 100. Sequential artifacts 095, 096, 097 and 098 are present; no GAH-GROK-099 or GAH-GROK-100 files are present in the audited workspace. A recommended_099_focus field is a recommendation, not proof that sprint 099 ran.

095 — handoff / ledger

GROK 095 marks the handoff out of the phone-story factory and into the accountability phase. The surviving footprint includes SURVIVOR-LEDGER.csv and EVIDENCE.json. Protected identities remain protected; this public page does not expand them.

096 — source seeds

GROK 096 carries the next phase through SURVIVOR-SEEDS.csv. Source-native review replaced phone-graph discovery, and private identity reconstruction stayed outside scope.

097 — source chronologies

GROK 097 advances the phase through SURVIVOR-CHRONOLOGIES.csv. Separate protected lanes remain separate unless a public source explicitly establishes an identity join.

098 — gap closure and legal-status repair

GROK 098 is the highest sequential artifact currently present. Its closeout opened the 2019 termination order (DE 478) and Jane Doe 1 declaration (DE 361-26), preserved the Jane Doe 3 joinder denial, the Eleventh Circuit en banc denial of mandamus, and the Supreme Court certiorari denial. It also preserved the February 2019 district CVRA finding as a historical district finding, not a final appellate merits holding.

098 records zero protected-identity expansions and zero new phone-story seeds. Two gaps remained explicit: the native June 7 Jane Doe 1 letter image and the year of the Jane Doe 2 letter.

Stop point. This recovered collection stops at 098 because that is where the files stop. GAH will not backfill 099 or 100 from a recommendation field, remembered chat state, or narrative continuity.

Protection standard

The public collection will name a survivor only when the relevant lane has a source-native public identity basis and naming adds necessary context. Protected or redacted lanes remain safe-labeled. Raw identifying material, inferred identities, and defeated redactions are outside the publication model.
