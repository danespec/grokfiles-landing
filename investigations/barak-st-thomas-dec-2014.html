St. Thomas, December 2014: The Trip and the Ticket in Another Man's Name

Ehud and Nili Barak spent four days hosted by Jeffrey Epstein in St. Thomas. The only airline itinerary for that trip in Barak's own email names a passenger who appears nowhere else in seven years of his mail. The documents, the anomaly, and both readings — labeled.

01 The story in one paragraph

December 2014: Epstein invites Ehud and Nili Barak to his Caribbean island. The Baraks accept, propose their own flights, and are hosted for four days — island lunch included — while Epstein also invites Larry Summers and Tom Pritzker to join. The visit is documented in Epstein’s staff emails and the Baraks’ mailbox; the invitations to Summers and Pritzker do not establish that either attended. But the only airline itinerary for that trip located in the indexed Barak files names a passenger who otherwise does not appear in seven years of that indexed email: “COHEN/RONEN MR,” booked on the Baraks’ exact proposed inbound flight, under a travel-agency subject line that — according to the supplied mailbox audit — uniquely names no passenger at all. Sources: leak id 18414 · leak id 18310 · leak id 19004 · leak id 18243 · DOJ EFTA00356456 · DOJ EFTA02596796 · DOJ EFTA02346686

Evidence boundary. The completed visit is documented. The identity behind the airline ticket remains unresolved. A proposal is not proof of travel; the Wallace thread establishes the completed visit. This page concerns a trip and a ticket and accuses Barak of no crime.

Audit scope. The 31,718-message index, 41 “St Thomas” hits, 1,380,648-document DOJ search and photo sweep below are findings reported in Ren’s three-crew research pass of October 21, supplied by Thomas on October 24, 2026. They are scoped search results, not proof that records or people do not exist. The separate raw-mail archive described in the Qatar investigation contains 40,976 HTML messages; those are different inventories and must not be conflated.

02 The documented sequence

  1. December 5, 2014 — the invitation

    Nili Priell Barak emails Epstein: Ehud will be in New York December 9–13; is Epstein in town? Epstein replies the same day: “Unfortunately not I'm in caribean” — then: “Come to Carib ?” Sources: leak id 18414

  2. December 6 — the plan

    Nili accepts and sketches roughly 48 hours over the weekend of December 12–13, returning on the 14th via Miami and New York to Israel. This is the early proposed schedule. Sources: leak id 18414

  3. December 7 — the flight, proposed to Epstein

    Nili sends the detailed version, subject “St Thomas”: “we can arrive to St. Thomas Friday, December 12 from NY at 12:55 PM with AA 1275 And leave on Monday, December 15 at 2 PM to NY with AA 1275. Does this suit you?” Epstein replies the same evening: “sounds great. i will lhave lesely coordinate” — assigning his assistant Lesley Groff. These are the flight numbers as written in the email, not an independently reconciled airline schedule. Sources: leak id 18310 · leak id 18018

  4. December 7, morning — the booking channel

    Hadassim Travel, the Baraks’ Tel Aviv agency, through agent “Yael,” sends Nili “Trip Details : TEST ( 12 Dec 2014 ).” The surviving body shows two prices ($1,020; $1,700) in encoding-damaged Hebrew and names no passenger. The supplied audit reports that every other Hadassim booking in this indexed mailbox names its passenger in the subject line — for example, “Trip Details : 1.BARAK/EHUD MR” (leak id 25238). This one says “TEST.” Its relationship to the ticket is an interpretation of the booking sequence, not a recovered agency-account join. Sources: leak id 18243 · leak id 25238

  5. December 9 — “or do you go by something else?”

    Epstein’s office arranges the hotel. Lesley Groff tells Nili: “Cecile in our St. Thomas office will make your reservations for you and Ehud… do you wish us to book the room under the name ‘Ehud Barak’? or do you go by something else?” Nili replies: “Under the name Nili priell is better. Thanks.” Groff: “Ok thank you. We will book under your name.” The hotel-booking exchange therefore places the room under Nili’s name rather than Ehud’s. It is not a recovered hotel folio. Sources: DOJ EFTA00356456

    In the same exchange Nili confirms: “We arrive at Thomas at 12:55 pm on Friday December 12 with AA 1275 And leave on Monday December 15 at 2 pm with AA 1275” and says: “When I get the hotel confirmation, I pass it to the security who reserves its own room on their expenses, near by.” A security detail was part of the travel arrangements and was to book its own nearby room at its own expense.

  6. December 11 — the gathering

    At 5:51 AM Epstein writes Tom Pritzker: “ehud on island for weekend, come visit.” The same week he invites Larry Summers for the island weekend. These records establish invitations, not attendance by Pritzker or Summers. Sources: DOJ EFTA02596796 · DOJ EFTA02346686

  7. December 12–15 — the visit, completed

    Epstein’s island staffer Daphne Wallace records the visit contemporaneously. On December 12, Nili writes “We arrived safely…” Wallace welcomes them at the Ritz-Carlton, St. Thomas: “He has arranged a light late lunch for you on his island this afternoon. Jermaine is waiting out front of the hotel…” On December 13, Nili writes a friend, “we are in the Caribbean.” On December 15 at 2:50 PM, Wallace sends a farewell: “I hope your visit was a good one. Mr. Epstein truly enjoyed this weekend.” This arrival, hospitality and farewell thread, rather than the earlier proposal, establishes the completed visit and arranged island lunch. Sources: DOJ EFTA00356456 · leak id 15729

  8. The ticket — a different passenger name

    A headerless attachment record in Barak’s indexed mailbox contains passenger COHEN/RONEN MR, PNR 7HLSOQ, AA reference YGVYVP. The supplied research identifies it as the only December 2014 passenger itinerary in that index and the only St. Thomas passenger record among its 41 “St Thomas” hits. It lists AA 1275, JFK → St. Thomas, Friday December 12, arriving 12:55 PM — the Baraks’ proposed inbound flight to the minute. Return: Sunday December 14, St. Thomas → Miami (AA 2228), Miami → London (AA 038, First), then El Al LY 316 London → Tel Aviv, December 17. An itinerary is a booking record, not proof of boarding or of the traveler’s identity beyond its printed name. Sources: leak id 19004

  9. February 2, 2015 — the echo

    Epstein asks Barak for “the name of your body guard when in st thomas”; the name in the thread is Jarrod Nadel. Epstein’s own recollection treats Barak’s St. Thomas stay, including a bodyguard, as a shared event. This does not establish that there was only one security officer. Sources: leak id 14998

03 The anomaly register

  1. Zero footprint

    “Ronen Cohen” appears in no other email in the reviewed leak index — never a sender, recipient or mention — across 31,718 indexed messages. The other Ronens (Bechor, Bergman, Lefler) and Cohens (Uri, Doron, David, Sender, Ronald) were assessed as different, documented people. This is an exact-name search finding; spelling variants, incomplete indexing and people absent from correspondence remain possible.

  2. Zero in the releases

    The supplied audit reports no hits for “Ronen Cohen” in its DOJ corpus of 1,380,648 documents. This is not a finding that no such person exists.

  3. Exact match

    Cohen’s inbound flight, date and arrival minute equal the itinerary Nili proposed to Epstein for the Baraks. Sources: leak id 19004 · leak id 18310

  4. Shape match

    His return — out on the 14th via Miami — matches Nili’s December 6 sketch, before the December 7 revision the Baraks ultimately followed; the Baraks stayed to December 15. Sources: leak id 18414 · leak id 19004 · DOJ EFTA00356456

  5. The “TEST” subject

    The booking delivery breaks the agency’s naming convention reported in the audit, in the booking central to this inquiry. “TEST” does not by itself identify a passenger or prove an alias. Sources: leak id 18243 · leak id 25238

  6. Principal-grade routing

    Business in, First out, El Al First home: the research reads this as a principal’s ticket rather than a staffer’s. That is a role inference from the fare classes, not proof of who traveled or a rule excluding security personnel. Sources: leak id 19004

  7. No Barak-named paper

    No St. Thomas itinerary under Barak’s own name was located for this trip in the reviewed index, although the visit is confirmed by two source families: the Barak leak and Epstein staff email in the DOJ release. Sources: leak id 18310 · leak id 15729 · DOJ EFTA00356456

  8. Documented name-avoidance on the same trip

    For the hotel, Epstein’s office explicitly offered a non-Barak booking name (“or do you go by something else?”), and Nili chose hers. The research interprets this as keeping Ehud Barak’s name off the hotel reservation where the couple controlled it. The choice is documented; a concealment motive or a matching airline alias is not established by that choice. Sources: DOJ EFTA00356456

04 The two readings

Reading A · Thomas’s hypothesis · Unproven identification

A separate travel identity

Thomas’s hypothesis is that Barak traveled the commercial leg under a separate identity — “Ronen Cohen” — so no airline manifest would carry his name into Epstein’s island gateway. It explains the anomaly register economically: the exact flight, zero footprint, “TEST” subject, class of travel and absence of a Barak-named ticket for a trip he provably took. This is an inference, not a passenger identification established by a manifest, passport, boarding record or agency account.

Reading B · Alternative explanation · Unproven identification

A real associate on a parallel itinerary

Ronen Cohen could be a real associate — security, advance or staff — whose trip paralleled the Baraks’ on the same flight, while Barak’s own-name ticket was booked through a channel whose records never entered this mailbox. This explains the return-leg mismatch: Cohen’s booking leaves December 14; the Baraks stayed to December 15. Reading A must treat Cohen’s earlier return as unused or changed, without a record establishing that change.

Reading B draws real support from the December 9 exchange: a security detail demonstrably accompanied the Baraks’ arrangements and self-booked nearby, so Cohen could be that detail’s paper. Against it, the February 2015 email names the St. Thomas bodyguard as Jarrod Nadel, not Cohen; the research regards security traveling First class home as atypical; and a working protection officer leaving no other trace in 31,718 indexed emails — no located roster, expense thread or cc — remains an anomaly. None of those points excludes a second officer, another staff role, an unrelated same-flight passenger or incomplete mailbox coverage.

What the archive can publish today is the sequence and the anomaly, in full, with both readings labeled. The identification itself is an inference — a strong one, argued from seven documented facts — and GAH’s standard governs its framing: “I would rather downgrade a promising story after checking the evidence than publish an exciting interpretation that the documents cannot sustain.” The documents are the story; the conclusion is the reader’s to draw alongside ours, labeled as ours.

“Strong” describes Thomas’s assessment, not an independently verified identification. The register above lists eight observations, including the additional hotel-name exchange. Neither reading is established as fact.

05 Photo hunt — negative finding

The supplied research reports that two crews searched for photographs of Barak in St. Thomas or on Epstein’s island and found none in the reviewed DOJ corpus or GAH photo collection. It also reports that no reviewed email mentioning St. Thomas, St. John, Caribbean or Virgin Islands carried a photographic image. Within that sweep, the trip window is photographically empty: the trip record presented here is text-only. An unsuccessful search is not proof that no photograph exists elsewhere.

Excluded image: the November 1, 2014 Bahamas sailing-catamaran photograph, with EXIF GPS east of Nassau, is not St. Thomas, is not Epstein’s island and has no established Epstein tie. It is not evidence for this trip and is not used on this page or its social card.

06 What would settle it

  • Any Groff/office logistics naming the December 12 arrival party. EFTA00356456 partially covers this by naming the Baraks at the hotel, but does not identify the airline passenger.
  • Hadassim’s account records for PNR 7HLSOQ: who paid, and for whom the “TEST” booking was opened.
  • Any second Cohen itinerary or Barak alias candidate in the raw leak attachments, with provenance and identity corroboration.
  • Independent records of Barak’s whereabouts December 12–14, 2014: press, Knesset or lecture records.
