Thesis

The archive should treat unresolved source slots as a reporting agenda, not as an argument.

Open questions are not filler. They are an editorial discipline. In the Epstein death file, missing context is tempting because silence feels meaningful. The archive standard is stricter: name the silence, show the attempted source path, and do not convert the gap into a hidden fact.

Counterevidence / alternate interpretation

What could be read another way

An alternate approach would hide open slots to make the site feel more complete. That would improve polish but damage trust. Visible uncertainty is part of the product.

Limitations

What this page does not prove

  • This page does not add new source records.
  • It does not claim that missing records were destroyed or hidden.
  • It does not infer bad faith from silence.
  • It does not replace official records requests or court-file searches.
