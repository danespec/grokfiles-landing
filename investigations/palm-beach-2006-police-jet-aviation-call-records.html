Palm Beach Police’s 2006 Evidence Chain: Grand Jury Scheduling and Jet Aviation Call Records

Original detective narratives and a May 1 sworn affidavit describe a postponed grand-jury session and the comparison of aircraft arrivals with telephone records. They are investigative records, not a fresh judicial finding.

Grok Archive Hub Editorial Desk · October 16, 2026 · Verified original records; investigation ongoing

Read source evidenceAll investigations

What these records establish. A detective's April 10, 2006 police narrative reports a postponed grand-jury session; a sworn May 1, 2006 affidavit says investigators obtained Jet Aviation records and compared 2005 aircraft arrivals with telephone communications. They do not independently prove why prosecutors postponed a session, reproduce the underlying manifests or call records, or establish misconduct by any lawyer.

April 10: a detective recorded a change in grand-jury scheduling

HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_019034 includes an April 10, 2006 entry attributed to Detective Joseph Recarey in Palm Beach Police incident report 1-05-000368. Recarey said a grand-jury session requested in February had been postponed after meetings with the State Attorney's Office and defense attorney Alan Dershowitz. His entry described a defense-provided packet of social-media material about witnesses and a request by prosecutors for time to review it.

The document records Recarey's account. It is not a transcript of the prosecutors' internal deliberations and does not prove that one lawyer alone made or improperly influenced the decision. The original released House page is available as a scanned image at HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_019034 (independent archive mirror). Its page label is a source locator, not a court conclusion. Additional excerpts from the Palm Beach Police report appear in a later federal civil-court exhibit; this source is not represented as the complete police case file. Court-filed Giuffre v. Maxwell police-report exhibit (access may require DOJ age verification).

May 1: the affidavit links flight-arrival and telephone evidence

In a May 1, 2006 sworn probable-cause affidavit, also seen at HOUSE_OVERSIGHT_018915, Recarey said subpoenaed 2005 Jet Aviation records supplied the dates on which Epstein's private aircraft arrived at and departed Palm Beach International Airport. He said investigators compared those dates with telephone records and found that certain calls tended to occur in proximity to aircraft presence in Palm Beach. A redacted copy appears in Government of the U.S. Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase, S.D.N.Y. ECF 238-16, filed July 25, 2023, page 23 of 25. Open the court-filed sworn affidavit, PDF page 23.

The affidavit is strong evidence of what analysis investigators reported performing; the underlying phone records, plane manifests and their complete comparison table are not reproduced in this source. Therefore GAH cannot independently calculate match rates, determine every flight's purpose or identify every person associated with the records.

What the evidence does not prove

Police reports and sworn affidavits are evidence of investigative findings and allegations at their time, not a later jury verdict. This article does not make a new accusation against defense lawyers or identify victims and witnesses behind redactions. Presence on a flight log, a telephone contact or an aircraft-arrival date does not by itself establish a crime.

Separate investigative timeframes matter: the 2005 aircraft log referenced here is unrelated without additional proof to Leon Black family-office aircraft-management plans discussed in 2015–17 emails. A matching company name such as Jet Aviation is not a shared transaction identifier.

Records needed next

  1. Complete February–April 2006 State Attorney grand-jury calendar and correspondence documenting reasons for postponement.
  2. Original subpoena returns for Jet Aviation and authenticated, privacy-protected call-detail records.
  3. Independent comparison of arrival/departure dates and call timestamps, suppressing victim identities and identifying itineraries.
  4. Source-by-source reconciliation with DOJ OPR's later account of prosecutorial decisions and the 2007 non-prosecution agreement.

Current status: Detective narrative and sworn affidavit verified as released records; causal decision chain and underlying aircraft/phone datasets remain unavailable for independent reconstruction.
