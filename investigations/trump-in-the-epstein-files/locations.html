Location Ledger: What Each Place Establishes and Does Not Establish

The same property appearing in multiple records is not proof of interaction or misconduct. This layer distinguishes alleged rooms, deposition locations, contact-directory locations, press context, and simple correspondence strings.

Location Rule

Do not treat presence at the same property as proof of interaction or misconduct. Addresses are used only when the source itself makes them public and the location is editorially necessary.
