Thesis

Watch-status records matter, but they need source-line discipline because they can show status context without explaining motive or knowledge by themselves.

The archive's late-July watch-status lane is a useful test of claim control. Readers may want it to answer why a status changed, whether a risk was understood, or whether later events were foreseeable. Counterevidence / alternate interpretation

What could be read another way

An alternate reading is that any watch-status record is automatically high-significance. The archive does not accept that shortcut. Status records can be relevant and still silent about why decisions were made.

Limitations
