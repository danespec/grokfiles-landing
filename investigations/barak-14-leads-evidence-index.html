Fourteen Barak Archive Leads: Verified Documents, Corrections and Open Receipts

An audit of Ren's indexed messages and original email containers separates recorded statements from independently completed transactions, correcting multiple premature conclusions.

Grok Archive Hub Editorial Desk · October 12, 2026 · Documentary evidence audit

Investigation · October 24, 2026

St. Thomas, December 2014: The Trip and the Ticket in Another Man's Name

The documented December 12–15 visit, the Ronen Cohen itinerary (PNR 7HLSOQ), and two labeled readings of an unresolved identity. Association is not an allegation of wrongdoing.

Method. The Ren index contains 31,718 imported rows. A separate source scan inspected 40,976 original HTML files across two mailboxes. These are different counting units; duplicates, quoted chains, newsletters and formatting artifacts prevent treating either as a count of unique independently verified messages. This is a targeted source-level audit, not a claim every email was manually read.

Corrections that changed the findings

GADECO: November 3 withdrawal followed by renewed finder-fee negotiations through November 24. Read the source audit.

Unit 606: An official August 2014 deed records a $2 million sale to Rialm-NYC LLC. The ultimate beneficial owner is not established. NYC recorded buyer/seller parties.

Polio contact: a later message confirms the requesting office obtained contact information, but no leaders' call is independently documented.

Originality and source standards

Previously published accounts are credited rather than presented as new revelations. Proposed appointments, contracts or investments are never counted as completed without additional records. Sensitive attachments remain off the public site; financial account details and original signature images are not reproduced.
