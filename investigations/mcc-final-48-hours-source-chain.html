The Final 48 Hours at MCC: A Source Chain, Not a Theory

The useful public question is not which theory sounds complete. It is which timeline claims can be tied to source cards, which claims depend on official summaries, and which intervals still sit in open receipt slots.

Thesis

The useful public question is not which theory sounds complete. It is which timeline claims can be tied to source cards, which claims depend on official summaries, and which intervals still sit in open receipt slots.

This reconstruction starts from the archive's existing Epstein death evidence lane and the already published final-48-hours research page. It treats the records as a chain: official report, source-card render, file-window pointer, watch-status record, exhibit-list pointer, and open slot. That chain can support a careful reading of sequence and uncertainty. It cannot support a finished theory by itself.

Counterevidence / alternate interpretation

What could be read another way

An alternate reading is that the public records show administrative failure and incomplete record preservation rather than a hidden causal explanation. The archive should preserve that possibility because the same source cards that support sequence also leave intent, motive, and some timing questions unanswered.

Limitations

What this page does not prove

  • This page does not inspect private source files or databases.
  • It does not infer video content from file names.
  • It does not turn an official summary into a complete primary-source reconstruction.
  • It does not claim who knew what at the time.
Open questions

What still needs source work

  • Which public source best fixes exact discovery, alarm, EMS, hospital, and pronouncement times?
  • Which public source can confirm camera continuity rather than only file-window existence?
  • Which source resolves cell-status and watch-status transitions at source-line level?
Why it matters

Reader value

The final-48-hours story is one of the highest-risk parts of the archive. If it is written as a theory page, it becomes generic and overconfident. If it is written as a source chain, readers can see both the evidence and the silence.
