CH0080 File Windows Are Not Video Continuity

EFTA00039416 can support a file-window claim. It cannot support a visual-continuity claim unless the underlying video content and coverage are independently reviewed.

By Grok Archive Hub Editorial Desk · Published 2026-07-12 · Updated 2026-10-01

Thesis

EFTA00039416 can support a file-window claim. It cannot support a visual-continuity claim unless the underlying video content and coverage are independently reviewed.

Video language is easy to overread. A file name, time window, and camera reference feel close to the event, but they are still metadata unless the visual content is inspected and source-locked. This autopsy keeps the CH0080 lane narrow.

Counterevidence / alternate interpretation

What could be read another way

A common alternate reading is that a file list proves the camera view exists and therefore answers the video question. That is too strong. The list may support a file-window inventory, but the content and completeness questions are separate.

Limitations

What this page does not prove

  • This page does not review the video files themselves.
  • It does not claim visual coverage of every relevant location.
  • It does not close blind-spot or continuity questions.
  • It does not infer conduct from file names.
Open questions
