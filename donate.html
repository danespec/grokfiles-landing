Funding status and public accounting

Monthly funding target: not yet published. We are reconciling actual infrastructure invoices before publishing a numerical goal. We will not display an invented progress bar, donation total, or expense figure.

Once verified, our public monthly summary will show the reporting period, operating-cost categories, aggregate contributions, processing or platform fees, expenses paid, and any carried-forward balance. Private donor information, account identifiers, security configuration, and sensitive invoices will not be published.

Amounts will be identified as verified, estimated, or pending reconciliation. We will disclose material sponsorships and financial relationships when relevant to coverage.

Independence and funding boundaries

Contributions do not buy favorable coverage, an investigation result, exclusion or removal of public evidence, access to sensitive personal material, or influence over an editorial correction. Any future paid advertisements will be labeled as advertisements, separated from source records, and subject to content and security review.

Interested in member benefits instead? See Patreon membership and the verified member portal. To verify our published work, read the methodology or submit a correction.

For source tips, broken links, or questions, use our contact page. Do not submit illegal material, private personal data, passwords, or information requiring privileged legal handling.
