Archive scope · updated October 6, 2026

The public site is the proof layer—not the size of the research corpus.

Grok Archive Hub publishes a deliberately smaller, reviewable evidence layer on top of a much larger indexed research corpus and retained research workspaces. We separate indexed research records, curated public evidence files, source holdings, mirrors, OCR/transcript derivatives, and working artifacts instead of adding unlike layers together into one marketing number.

Counting rule: GAH does not count every OCR text file, transcript segment, image derivative, checksum, working export, backup, or mirrored copy as a separate primary document. The figures below describe different layers and are intentionally not summed.

Three different scale layers

Indexed research corpus

1.38M+ indexed research records

This is the broad discovery layer used to locate and cross-check leads. It is an index-level corpus figure, not a claim that every indexed record is a unique primary-source file.

Published proof layer

3,628 curated public evidence files

This is the deliberately smaller reader-verification layer surfaced by GAH. It should not be read as the size of the underlying research corpus.

Deployment

3,881 allowlisted public files

The current production package contains 3,881 explicitly allowlisted files. Of those, 3,652 are evidence-data assets rather than general site code or page chrome.

Evidence assets

Multiple source formats

The deployed evidence layer currently includes 93 PDFs, 2,457 VTT transcript files, 415 text files, 349 JSON files, and 270 image assets. These are formats, not additive document claims.

What sits behind the indexed and public layers

The retained research environment is materially larger than the public deployment, but filesystem holdings and search-index records measure different things. The retained workspaces contain overlapping mirrors, working copies, OCR products, frozen research runs, and private review material. GAH therefore does not add the 1.38M+ index figure to filesystem counts or present the sum as a unique-document total.

Why the numbers are not additive

  • Mirrors overlap. A source PDF may exist in a source mirror, a research workspace, a frozen review package, and a deployment package without becoming four documents.
  • OCR is derived. OCR text, VTT transcripts, extracted JSON, page images, and indexes help locate evidence; they do not replace the underlying source.
  • Research runs multiply artifacts. Manifests, checksums, review ledgers, logs, and intermediate exports are provenance tools, not source-document inflation.
  • Private review is not public proof. Retained material can remain private or unpromoted until provenance, privacy, legal, and evidentiary review is complete.

What GAH is optimizing for

Traceability over volume

A smaller public set with provenance, source links, claim boundaries, and correction paths is more useful than an undifferentiated file dump.

Original cross-checks

GAH uses the retained corpus to connect filings, emails, financial records, timelines, oversight actions, and contradictions—then publishes the narrowest conclusion the records support.

Public proof stays public

Membership supports storage, acquisition, digitization, OCR, source review, and reporting. It does not turn stronger accusations or essential proof into a paywalled product.

Verification note

The public deployment and retained-workspace figures below were checked against the production allowlist and NAS inventory. The 1.38M+ figure is an index-level research-corpus count and is intentionally kept separate from unique-source, deployment-file, and filesystem counts. File counts from permission-restricted trees are minimum directly enumerable counts, not estimates of inaccessible material. Storage footprints and counts can change as mirrors are deduplicated, new sources are acquired, and research runs are frozen or removed.
