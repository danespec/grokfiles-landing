PAUL A. ENGELMAYER, District Judge:  
The Court has been forwarded, by the chambers of the Hon. Richard M. Berman, the 
attached letter dated February 1, 2026, from counsel for victims of Jeffrey Epstein and Ghislaine 
Maxwell.  See Ex. A.  With this order, the Court is docketing the letter, which counsel for the 
victims evidently intended to be docketed in this case.1  The letter states that materials recently 
made public by the Department of Justice (“DOJ”) pursuant to the Epstein Files Transparency 
Act (“EFTA”) contain “thousands” of instances in which victim identifying information was 
errantly not redacted.  Id. at 2.  It further states that DOJ, despite having been notified by 
victims’ counsel of the need for such redactions, had not yet done so, such that the unredacted 
materials remained publicly accessible via DOJ’s online repository of Epstein files.  Id. at 3. 
These concerns appear to have been addressed, at least in part.  Yesterday, DOJ filed a 
letter response.  Dkt. 848.  DOJ’s letter stated that “several thousands of documents and media 
[that DOJ had made public] may have inadvertently included victim identifying information,” id.
