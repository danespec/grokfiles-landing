Terms

Use the archive carefully.

Grok Archive Hub is a public records and research navigation project. Membership supports the work; it does not buy conclusions, legal advice, hidden evidence, or editorial outcomes.

Plain-English terms: use public records responsibly, keep claims tied to sources, and do not treat archive presence as proof of guilt, conduct, affiliation, identity, attendance, purpose, or legal status.

Public archive access

Public evidence pages, source routes, SEO stories, primary-source pages, dispatches, corrections, methodology, and search remain public unless a route is unavailable for technical, legal, safety, or privacy reasons.

Membership

Patreon is the checkout and billing system for membership. This site verifies Patreon membership status for protected member routes but does not collect payment-card or bank data.

Member benefits may include early research releases, downloadable evidence ledgers, cleared source packets, operations briefings, investigation voting, and prioritized archive/source requests. Benefits are bounded by source safety, privacy review, and public-proof rules.

No purchase of conclusions

Support or membership does not purchase a conclusion, favorable coverage, source removal, takedown, private claim, accusation, legal opinion, or priority over correction evidence.

Source requests

Requests should include a public URL, exact wording or source identifier, and the durable record context to check. Do not submit illegal material, private personal data, passwords, payment data, threats, or anything requiring secure legal handling.

Availability

The archive may change routes, pause member access, remove unsafe material, correct records, or deny access when membership is inactive, unavailable, canceled, wrong-tier, technically unverifiable, or unsafe to process.

Contact

For corrections, source tips, membership questions, or broken-link reports, use the public contact path. Do not send secrets or payment credentials.
