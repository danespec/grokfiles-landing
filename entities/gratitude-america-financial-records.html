Gratitude America: Documented Account Transfers and Unresolved Questions

This dossier concerns one charity’s released banking and investigative records, not a finding of criminal conduct. It distinguishes the transfer into an identified money-market account, movements between the charity’s own accounts, and questions for which the underlying records remain missing.

Evidence boundary: An organization appearing in these records is not evidence of guilt. Every entry identifies its source and separates what the document supports from what remains unknown. This page does not identify protected victims or witnesses.

Documentary timeline and source trail

  1. October 14, 2015

    EFTA00080260 records a $10 million incoming transfer from BV70 LLC into a Gratitude America money-market account.

    Source: Bank-derived Exhibit E

  2. 2016 account movements

    The same single-account exhibit lists $8.775 million of outgoing postings: $8.45 million to other accounts owned by Gratitude America and $325,000 to outside recipients. Internal transfers are not grants.

    Source: 28-row source reconstruction

  3. 2020 inquiry

    EFTA00153569–571 records a French financial-intelligence inquiry and a reference to three suspicious-activity reports, without supplying the SAR texts or a disposition.

    Source: Released correspondence

  4. April 2021

    EFTA00128841 shows that an IRS contract accountant reviewed bank records within an FBI matter; the import form does not include the conclusions of that review.

    Source: FBI import form

What these records do not establish

  • The released account schedule is not a full set of monthly statements and does not reconstruct every account or recipient.
  • Internal account-to-account transfers must not be mislabeled charitable grants or external payments.
  • A French inquiry and references to SARs are not proof of a criminal charge or a finding against a recipient.
  • Underlying ledgers, referenced SARs, receipt-level wire evidence and the accountant’s full report remain unavailable in the reviewed public records.
