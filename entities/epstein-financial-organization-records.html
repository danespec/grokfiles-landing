Epstein Financial Entities: Research Directory

This directory covers organizations and legal entities in existing published financial research. A name appearing here is not an allegation of criminal activity. Entity, account, transaction and ownership are separate research fields.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Documentary roles and sources

  1. JPMorgan Chase

    Open source-linked dossier

    Documented research lane: Bank reporting and forensic-accounting record.

    Source path and boundary: Nine-SAR analysis; EFTA02810827. Reported suspicious activity is not a verdict.

  2. Deutsche Bank

    Open source-linked dossier

    Documented research lane: Banking relationship, compliance and financial exhibits.

    Source path and boundary: Interview records; EFTA00080260. Interview accounts and a bank-derived exhibit are distinct.

  3. Gratitude America Ltd.

    Open source-linked dossier

    Documented research lane: Charity money-market account and separate account trails.

    Source path and boundary: Account reconstruction; separate FirstBank account. Do not count same-entity transfers as external grants.

  4. BV70 LLC

    Documented research lane: Identified origin of a credit in the Gratitude America exhibit.

    Source path and boundary: EFTA00080260. A credit does not establish all subsequent uses or ultimate ownership.

  5. Southern Trust

    Documented research lane: Investment administration and FirstBank account records.

    Source path and boundary: Execution-layer review; FirstBank chronology. A shared administrator is not proof of common beneficial ownership.

  6. Financial Trust / Black Family Partners

    Documented research lane: Separate transaction stages in ESWW sale and repricing records.

    Source path and boundary: Sale analysis; repricing. Draft and closing figures are not interchangeable.

  7. Apollo / Dechert review

    Documented research lane: Commissioned review of Leon Black’s relationship and payments.

    Source path and boundary: SEC-filed review; scope comparison. Do not attribute an individual’s payment to Apollo without a supporting record.

  8. Reporty / Carbyne

    Documented research lane: Investment and business-development records.

    Source path and boundary: Investment brief; business-development chain. A proposal and a completed investment are different claims.

  9. Kyara I

    Documented research lane: Unresolved underlying holding in an investment vehicle.

    Source path and boundary: Open identification question. Do not infer an asset from a vehicle name.

  10. Neoteny 3

    Documented research lane: Fund-subscription record.

    Source path and boundary: Subscription scope. Fund subscription does not establish ownership of each portfolio company.

Entity-resolution checklist

Identify the legal name, jurisdiction where established, dated instrument, account holder and transaction direction. Do not merge similarly named trusts or entities, or treat a shared contact, administrator or investment platform as proof of a common cash flow. The existing shared-administration analysis documents why this distinction matters.
