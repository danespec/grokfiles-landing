Epstein Records: Public Institutions and Roles

This directory organizes public institutions by the actual type of record they issued or received. It does not attribute the conduct of individual personnel to every person in an institution.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Documentary roles and sources

  1. U.S. Department of Justice

    Documentary role: Federal investigation, disclosure and professional-responsibility review.

    Start with these sources and limits: OPR statement; disclosure portal. Separate the 2007–2008 decision record from later disclosures.

  2. Southern District of Florida U.S. Attorney’s Office

    Documentary role: 2007 agreement and victim-notification process.

    Start with these sources and limits: NPA source guide; EFTA00013764. Institutional participation does not prove the motive of every official.

  3. Southern District of New York U.S. Attorney’s Office

    Documentary role: 2019 federal criminal case.

    Start with these sources and limits: Atlas case chronology; court records guide. Charges and adjudicated outcomes must be distinguished.

  4. Federal Bureau of Prisons / MCC New York

    Documentary role: Detention operations and preservation records.

    Start with these sources and limits: EFTA00035570; EFTA00035035. Routine logs and hold notices have different evidentiary purposes.

  5. DOJ Office of the Inspector General

    Documentary role: Later investigation of custody, care and supervision.

    Start with these sources and limits: Official report; EFTA00039025. Review findings are distinct from unreviewed contemporaneous entries.

  6. Federal Bureau of Investigation

    Documentary role: Investigative holdings and review records.

    Start with these sources and limits: FBI Vault source audit. Release metadata does not establish record completeness.

  7. FinCEN / French financial-intelligence inquiry

    Documentary role: Financial-intelligence correspondence.

    Start with these sources and limits: EFTA00153569. A request for information or reference to a SAR is not a criminal finding.

  8. Senate Finance Committee

    Documentary role: Financial oversight and reported payment and SAR figures.

    Start with these sources and limits: SAR analysis; payment figures. Committee findings and judicial determinations are distinct.

  9. U.S. District Court / D.C. Circuit

    Documentary role: Procedural orders and filings in disclosure litigation.

    Start with these sources and limits: district-court tracker; appellate order. A party proposal is not an order.

Identity and institutional boundaries

An agency may publish a document written by another body; release does not equal authorship. A prosecutor, regulator, court and legislative committee have different authorities. Use the issuing institution and record date in a citation; do not infer a coordinated action from a shared institutional label.
