Epstein Records: Chronological Source Guide

A chronological source guide across GAH’s published legal, financial, travel and custody records: dated source paths, separate document and publication dates, and explicit gaps. A finding aid, not a complete chronology of every raw archive document.

Read the dates correctly. The sequence below uses dates of source records or events described in those records, not GAH publication dates. A scheduled event is not proof of attendance. Adjacent records are not causal proof.

How to use this guide

  1. Choose the dated record or research path.
  2. Open its primary receipt and inspect the page, edition and provenance.
  3. Separate document wording, later reporting and unresolved questions.
