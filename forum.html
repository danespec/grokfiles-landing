GAH Investigation Room · Public discussion beta

Bring the source. Debate the record.

The GAH public discussion community is live, hosted on GitHub Discussions. Readers can post source-check questions, answer open documentary requests, and challenge cited evidence. A free GitHub account is required to post. The discussion forum is not a GAH-hosted confidential or anonymous reporting channel.

Enter the live discussion forum →Read the rules and start here →Community Desk

How this forum operates: Discussions are public immediately on GitHub before human review. The GAH repository owner can moderate, close and lock conversations. Posts belong to their authors and are not GAH editorial findings. For the initial pilot, GitHub accounts less than 24 hours old are temporarily restricted until October 21, 2026 (GitHub's existing-users interaction limit, scheduled to expire automatically).

Privacy: Do not post information identifying anonymous survivors, private personal/account details, confidential or protected materials, or unsupported allegations of guilt. For ordinary public-source tips that should not appear in a discussion thread, use Contact / Source Tips. That email route is also not anonymous or end-to-end encrypted.

How to start a useful discussion

  1. Open the original public exhibit or court filing and identify the exact date, EFTA/ECF/Bates number and cited passage.
  2. Separate what the record literally says from hypothesis, uncertainty and alternative explanations.
  3. Choose a GitHub category: Q&A for a specific source check, General for broader documented findings, or Ideas for improving the archive.
  4. Protect private individuals and survivors. Do not upload protected SARs, private records or unredacted filings. Report privacy concerns to the operator through GAH Contact.

Note: A GitHub account, governed by GitHub's privacy rules, is necessary to create posts and comments. No thread is embedded directly on GAH. Do not infer from a contribution that GAH endorses its claims or has independently verified its evidence.
