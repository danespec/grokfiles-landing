Methodology · Source maps

How Source Maps Work

Source maps organize files, routes, receipts, entities, timelines, and review lanes. They are navigation structures, not accusations.

Back to MethodologyOpen Barak Source MapLive changelog

What this can show

  • Which source lane a record belongs to.
  • How receipt cards, entities, timelines, redactions, visual records, and review lanes connect.
  • Which records are source-linked and which still need open checks.
  • Where readers should go before forming a conclusion.

What this does not prove

  • A source map does not prove motive.
  • A related route does not prove conduct.
  • A name appearing in one lane does not connect it to another lane automatically.
  • A timeline entry does not prove causation.
  • A review lane is not an accusation.

How Grok Archive Hub uses this

The archive uses source maps to slow the reader down. Before asking what a record means, source maps ask what kind of record it is, where it came from, what confidence label applies, and what remains unresolved.
