How the verification works

  • Claim: The precise allegation, not an amplified version.
  • Source: The original released document and chronology.
  • Limit: What the evidence cannot establish.
  • Open receipt: The record that could change the answer.

No outrage shortcut

Our headlines pose the disputed question. The first paragraph answers it. We do not attribute wrongdoing from names, photographs, draft allegations or unexplained redactions.
