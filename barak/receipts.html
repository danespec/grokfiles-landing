A receipt card is a source-linked record summary. It tells readers what the archive can currently show, what confidence level applies, and what the record does not prove.

Reader Summary

This page indexes receipt cards, not accusations. Receipts are grouped by lane. Each receipt separates what the record shows from what it does not prove. Confidence labels prevent overreading. Open receipt slots are unresolved source checks. Presence in a receipt does not imply conduct.

The Receipts Index is the bridge between the Barak Source Map and the underlying archive. It lets readers move from method to records without turning proximity, partial exports, media appearances, or unresolved source gaps into conclusions.

Source discovery for this page used the existing public-safe Barak portal data file, not private-only review packets. Rows below are selected from the promoted public Barak index and link back to the Barak search/detail route.

Confidence Labels

L1

Source Found

A record exists and is linked.

L2

Context Supported

The record has enough surrounding context to explain why it matters.

L3

Review Lead

The record raises a structured question but does not prove the answer.

L4

Source-Locked Finding

Multiple durable sources support a narrow factual finding.

Open Receipt Slot

Unresolved source item

A missing, unresolved, ambiguous, degraded, or pending source item.

How to Use This Index

  1. Pick a receipt lane.
  2. Open the source record or search link.
  3. Check the confidence label.
  4. Read what the record shows.
  5. Read what the record does not prove.
  6. Follow open receipt slots before drawing conclusions.
  7. Send corrections or source tips if a durable source can resolve a slot.

What This Page Does Not Say

  • It does not say Ehud Barak violated FARA.
  • It does not say any person acted illegally.
  • It does not say presence in a record implies conduct.
  • It does not say an email proves agreement or intent.
  • It does not say a photo proves relationship depth.
  • It does not say a translation is final without review.
  • It does not convert open receipt slots into findings.
  • It does not replace DOJ, court, registration, or official records.
