How to request a check

Send durable records, not accusations.

Ask the archive to check specific records, dates, names, EFTA IDs, broken links, OCR conflicts, or correction history. A request is a lead until source-linked proof and confidence language are reviewed.

Contact / Source Tips: grokcloudflare@gmail.com

Send source tips, correction requests, broken-link reports, or archive questions. Do not send illegal material, private personal data, passwords, or anything requiring secure legal handling.
