Open slot types

Visual read

Unresolved name read

Rows, signatures, OCR, and handwriting candidates stay caveated until full-page context and source-locking are complete.

Context

Missing adjacent material

Facing pages, headers, room rosters, and continuity can change confidence and are tracked explicitly.

Durability

Degraded public links

Older reporting may disappear or degrade; durable records such as court filings, official reports, and archive pages carry more weight.
