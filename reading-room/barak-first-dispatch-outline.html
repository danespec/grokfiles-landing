Claim-Specific Dispatch Gate

For any future claim-specific Barak dispatch, require source-linked receipt IDs, identity disambiguation, timeline context, lane-specific caveats, open slots listed, legal-safety review, a “what this does not prove” section, durable source links if any legal/public-office/FARA point is discussed, no unsupported accusation, and no legal conclusion unless sourced to DOJ, court, or official records.

Rejection / Downgrade Rules

A candidate must be rejected or downgraded if the source link is missing, identity is ambiguous, translation is unresolved, timeline is unsupported, only proximity supports the theory, the claim requires motive inference, legal conclusion is unsupported, absence of registration is treated as misconduct, or an open slot is being treated as proof.
