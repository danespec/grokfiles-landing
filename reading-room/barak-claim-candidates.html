Required safety disclaimer: This page is a Reading Room working queue. It does not accuse any person or entity of wrongdoing. A claim candidate is not a finding, not a legal conclusion, and not a public claim.

The Claim Candidates Queue tracks possible future dispatch topics that still need source-locking, receipt review, translation checks, identity checks, timeline checks, and legal-safety review before publication.

Reader Summary

This queue is for candidates, not claims. Each candidate must be source-locked before becoming a public dispatch. Candidate topics may be rejected, merged, downgraded, or left open. The queue preserves uncertainty; nothing here should be treated as an accusation.

The queue exists to slow publication down. A topic can look interesting and still fail source-lock review. If the evidence does not support a narrow public finding, the candidate stays open, gets downgraded, or gets rejected.

A claim candidate is not a claim. It is a controlled workbench item for deciding what the archive must check before public-facing copy is written.

Open Checks

Identity, date, translation, source-chain, legal-safety, public proof, and “what this does not prove” checks.

Publication Gate

For the current lowest-risk starting point, open the Barak First Dispatch Outline. It recommends a method-and-receipt orientation page, not a named accusation or legal claim.

A candidate cannot become a public dispatch until it has source-linked receipt IDs, identity disambiguation, timeline context, lane-specific caveats, open slots listed, legal-safety review, a “what this does not prove” section, public proof links, and no unsupported accusation.

Source-linked receipt IDsEvery public point must trace back to an existing receipt card. Identity disambiguationNames and entities remain controlled before stronger wording is used. Timeline contextDate language stays separate from causation language. Lane-specific caveatsEmail, PDF, media, translation, and legal lanes carry different source limits.

Rejection Rules

A candidate must be rejected or downgraded if the source link is missing, identity is ambiguous, translation is unresolved, timeline is unsupported, only proximity supports the theory, the claim requires motive inference, a legal conclusion is unsupported, or an open slot is being treated as proof.

What This Page Does Not Say

  • It does not say Ehud Barak violated FARA.
  • It does not say any person or entity acted illegally.
  • It does not say a candidate is true.
  • It does not say a record appearance proves conduct.
  • It does not say proximity proves coordination.
  • It does not say absence of registration proves misconduct.
  • It does not convert open slots into findings.
  • It does not replace DOJ, court, registration, or official records.
