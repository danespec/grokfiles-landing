How a lead becomes verified research

  1. Locate: supply a public original URL and source ID, not a social screenshot alone.
  2. Context: identify the exact dated passage, page and who authored the record.
  3. Separate: label direct facts, inference, possible alternate interpretations and gaps.
  4. Review: GAH verifies, redacts sensitive details and independently decides what can be published.

Public records, corrections and editorial research do not require a paid GAH membership. A public forum will not be opened without moderation, abuse protection and clear rules.

Contributor standards and safety

  1. Evidence first; show original records and quote the narrow passage faithfully.
  2. No harassment, threats, survivor identification, doxxing or publishing private contact information.
  3. No illegally obtained records, secrets, unredacted confidential documents, bank credentials or files requiring secure legal intake.
  4. Do not treat mere mention in a record as evidence of guilt, contact or coordination.
  5. Tip submission does not guarantee a reply, review result, editorial adoption or publication.
  6. Email is not anonymous, end-to-end encrypted, or legally privileged. Do not use it for confidential or sensitive whistleblower submissions.

GAH does not offer SecureDrop, anonymous tip infrastructure, secure legal intake or confidential source protection. Privacy · Editorial policy · Corrections
