Follow the custody timeline—not the noise.

This dispatch is a reading path into the existing evidence page. It separates sourced chronology from interpretation and records unresolved silence without treating silence as proof.

Open the evidence pageSearch related records

Confidence note: confidence belongs to individual claims and records. The existence of conflict, omission, or silence does not itself prove a theory of cause or responsibility.

The reading sequence

  1. Step 1Establish custody and control pointsIdentify who held formal responsibility, when transitions occurred, and which facts are directly documented.
  2. Step 2Build the chronologyPlace medical, staffing, surveillance, housing, and discovery records on one timeline without filling gaps by assumption.
  3. Step 3Compare official accountsRecord where statements agree, differ, or rely on incomplete source material.
  4. Step 4Log the silenceState what the available record does not resolve. Silence remains an open question.
  5. Step 5Return to receiptsOpen the linked evidence cards and original source routes before adopting any conclusion.

What this dispatch does not claim

It does not declare a cause beyond adjudicated or directly sourced findings. It does not convert anomalies into proof. It does not assign guilt from presence, access, institutional role, or public speculation.
