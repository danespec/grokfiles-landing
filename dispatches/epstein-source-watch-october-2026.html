Epstein source watch: what is verified, pending, or unconfirmed

A date-stamped verification queue for high-attention Epstein-related stories. “Verified” means the source document or event exists; it does not mean every allegation in it is true. “Pending” means follow-up on a known source. “Unverified” claims are held, not amplified.

Editorial gate: Every headline must have provenance, publication date and evidentiary limits. Association does not establish guilt. A missing search result is not proof of removal. This is a curated snapshot, not an automatically refreshed feed.

verified · Signal 4 · ECF 59

Phang v. Blanche: DOJ reports October 16 in-camera delivery

In its October 16 ECF 59 Notice of Compliance, DOJ says it hand-delivered a binder at 10:55 a.m. Eastern, before the noon deadline under signed ECF 57. The binder reportedly included redacted handwritten FBI notes underlying FD-302s EFTA01245620, EFTA02858481 and EFTA02858491, submitted to the court for in-camera review, not publicly released. This is the government's affirmative notice of timely delivery, not judicial verification of the binder contents or approval of redactions.

DOJ says the notes duplicate typed FD-302s and requests time to consider an appeal if the court later orders public disclosure. Its notice does not grant a stay or order public release. ECF 42 separately required a certified administrative-record list filed with the court Oct. 1 and the full record delivered to the plaintiff Oct. 15; both remain independently unverified here. The signed disposition of the Oct. 1 ECF 51 postponement motion remains a separate open question. ECF 52 is a Rule 12(c) motion, not a merits judgment; ECF 55 is party opposition, not an order. The D.D.C. RSS and partial RECAP mirror are not a complete docket.
