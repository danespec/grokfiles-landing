Presence in a record is the beginning—not the conclusion.

Interpretation boundary: a scheduling reference, contact entry, invitation, calendar item, or co-occurrence can establish that a source contains the reference. It does not by itself establish purpose, knowledge, legal meaning, or wrongdoing.

Candidate lane

Open slots

Unrecovered sources, transcript needs, and unresolved context remain visible rather than being smoothed over.
