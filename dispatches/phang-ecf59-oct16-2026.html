Phang ECF 59: DOJ Reports Timely Delivery of Redacted FBI Notes

In a three-page notice dated October 16 (ECF 59), the Department of Justice reports hand-delivering a binder of redacted FBI handwritten notes to Judge Emmet G. Sullivan’s chambers at 10:55 a.m. Eastern, ahead of the court’s noon deadline.

Evidence boundary: This is DOJ's affirmative representation of timely delivery, not an independent judicial finding about the binder's contents, an order to disclose the notes publicly, or judicial approval of the redactions.

What the filing establishes

The signed October 9 ECF 57 order directed DOJ to submit redacted handwritten notes underlying three FBI FD-302 interview reports to the court for in-camera review by noon Eastern on October 16. ECF 59 identifies the notes underlying EFTA01245620, EFTA02858481, and EFTA02858491 and marked-for-redaction document stamps 3501.045-002, 3501.045-004, and 3501.045-006, respectively.

DOJ says it separately provided unredacted copies to the court on September 24. The government maintains that the handwritten notes duplicate already-released typewritten FD-302 reports, so it had not processed them for the public-facing Epstein Library. That is the government's litigating position; ECF 59 is not a court ruling that the notes may lawfully be withheld.

What has not happened

DOJ requests that, if the court later orders release, any such order be stayed to permit consideration of an appeal. ECF 59 requests a possible future stay; it grants none. No court order publishing these notes is established by this notice. The submission was for private judicial review, not immediate public disclosure.
