August 8 Attorney Log: The Colón Miró Lead

A source-first look at the August 8 BOP Attorney Conference Logbook row, the Colón visual read, the Colón Miró candidate match, and the open receipt slots that still need clean proof.

Open EFTA00035147 Read the evidence lane Search the archive

Core finding: The row visually reads Colón. Public records and reporting identify Mariel A. Colón Miró as an Epstein legal-team attorney connected to the August 8, 2019 will execution. The archive treats this as a high-confidence candidate match, not a forensic handwriting certainty.

What the row shows

The primary archive anchor is EFTA00035147, PDF page 15, with footer EFTA00035161. The page belongs to the BOP Attorney Conference Logbook chain.

On the target row, the visible fields read: date 8/8, name Colón, inmate Epstein, register number 76318-054, time in 8:30, and time out 12:30.

The row supports a narrow presence-and-log claim: a Colón entry aligns with Epstein / Reg# 76318-054 / 8:30–12:30 on 8/8. It does not, by itself, prove first name, employer, motive, misconduct, or meaning.

Why Colón Miró is the high-confidence candidate

The archive does not jump from a handwritten last name to a full identity. The candidate match comes from the alignment between the log row, official will context, and public source reporting.

Archive row

Colón appears beside Epstein.

EFTA00035147 page 15 / footer EFTA00035161 visually shows Colón aligned with Epstein, Reg# 76318-054, 8:30–12:30.

Official report

DOJ OIG places an attorney meeting and will signing on August 8.

DOJ OIG Report 23-085 states that Epstein met with attorneys at the prison on August 8, 2019 and signed a new will.

Court-filed will

Mariel A. Colón Miró appears in the will witness block.

The U.S. Virgin Islands court-filed Epstein will PDF identifies Mariel A. Colón Miró in the witness materials.

That combination supports Mariel A. Colón Miró as the high-confidence candidate for the Colón row. The confidence is high because the name, date, inmate, register number, will context, and public reporting point in the same direction. It is still caveated because the log row itself prints only the last name and the signature block is not treated as forensic proof.

What this does not prove

This dispatch does not claim motive, misconduct, conspiracy, or wrongdoing. It does not claim MCC staff knew will details. It does not claim handwriting certainty. It does not claim that adjacent FERRON-like rows identify Marc Fernich.

The archive is tracking a source-linked candidate match. Presence in a log row is not a conduct finding. A source record can show a name, date, inmate, register number, and time window while remaining silent about purpose, knowledge, and interpretation.

Correction history

Earlier drafts referred to this row as Colvin, then Mas Colón. After full-page review, the active read is Colón; Mas was a blended read from the adjacent signature block.

The current source-safe form is: the row visually reads Colón. The Colón Miró identification is a high-confidence candidate match built from row context, will records, public reporting, and the source review note ATTORNEY_LOG_SOURCE_REVIEW.md.
