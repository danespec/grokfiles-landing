Content safety

What this page does not do.

  • It does not verify unresolved evidence gaps.
  • It does not turn a review lead into a finding.
  • It does not imply guilt, conduct, knowledge, agency, coordination, or liability from record presence.
  • It does not treat redactions, visuals, chronology, names, or flight logs as proof by themselves.
  • It does not change the public proof standard or the claim language on story/evidence pages.
