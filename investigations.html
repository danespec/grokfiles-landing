Editorial map

The archive now separates story types.

Investigations carry the full claim package: original title, thesis, introduction, findings, source chain, timeline, alternate interpretation, limitations, open questions, why-it-matters note, byline, dates, schema, and direct evidence links. Evidence Briefs answer narrower questions. Document Autopsies inspect one source object. Timeline Reconstructions organize sequence without treating chronology as causation. The Contradiction Ledger keeps conflicts and missing elements visible. Open Questions hold unresolved receipt slots.

Reader route

Read first, inspect second.

Start with an investigation when you need the human-readable frame. Move to the linked source chain when you need the receipts. Use Explore the Archive for raw search, APIs, source cards, and machine-oriented tools after you know the claim you are testing.
