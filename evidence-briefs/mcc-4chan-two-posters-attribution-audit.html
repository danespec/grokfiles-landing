The MCC 4chan Record: Two Posters, One Attribution Overclaim

The August 10, 2019 4chan record is more precise than the viral retelling. The released FBI material shows two poster identities and two associated IPs; it does not, on its face, prove Roberto Grijalva authored either one.

Grok Archive Hub Editorial Desk · October 6, 2026

Correction. A prior GAH thread capture treated Roberto Grijalva as the identified 4chan poster. That formulation is too strong for the public source chain. The released grand-jury material establishes that the FBI pursued the posts and received records from 4chan; it also shows two distinct poster identities / associated IPs. The public bundle, on its face, does not prove Grijalva authored either poster ID.

Four posts, two poster identities

EFTA00133349 contains the FBI grand-jury subfile and 4chan's response. Report #2093 lists four post numbers and two associated IPs. The early death / medical-detail posts were made under the poster ID rPtND1Si. The separate "switch-out" claim was posted under swam8WIF. Treating those statements as one anonymous witness collapses two different source identities.

The subpoena chain

  1. August 10, 2019The early 4chan posts appear before the first major press reports. One poster supplies death and resuscitation details; a different poster later alleges Epstein had been moved and "switched out."
  2. August 13, 2019EFTA00123462. SDNY issues a grand-jury subpoena to 4chan listing the four post numbers.
  3. August 14, 2019EFTA00129268 / EFTA00133349. 4chan's response supplies the requested post data and associated IP information.
  4. Same dayEFTA00133182. The subpoena-tracking material records carrier follow-up for IP addresses from 4chan.

Where the Grijalva claim breaks

Roberto Grijalva appears in separate MCC records, including an internal post-death memo. Other pages in the large grand-jury production contain subscriber, carrier, and financial records. Physical adjacency inside a 274-page production is not an identity join. GAH does not infer "4chan author" from the presence of Grijalva-related material elsewhere in the same investigative bundle.

The narrow correction is attribution: two posters existed, and the currently public bundle does not justify collapsing either one into Roberto Grijalva without an explicit source join.
