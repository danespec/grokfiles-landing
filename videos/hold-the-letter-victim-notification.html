What this video examines

Although its YouTube title promotes the archive generally, the published description identifies a specific source trail: victim-notification letters and the December 7, 2007 hold instruction. This watch page connects that upload to the existing investigation rather than creating another investigation.

The central email is EFTA00013764. The original description’s EFTA01703108 reference belongs to separate charging-document context. It does not identify the hold email or establish an indictment’s filing status.

The later OPR report recounts the instruction and Sloman’s uncertainty about whom he consulted. The instruction is documentary evidence; the unresolved identity remains an open question. The report was written in November 2020 and the cited court copy was filed in July 2023.

DOJ’s public findings include both no professional-misconduct finding and criticism of judgment and victim treatment. This page carries that outcome beside the chronology without inferring a hidden official, intelligence explanation or adjudicated criminal intent.

What this does not prove

The December 2007 instruction is documented; the identity of the person Sloman recalled consulting is unresolved. A missing decision record is not proof of a secret order, intelligence involvement or criminal intent. The November 2020 report date, July 2023 court-filing date and December 2007 email date identify different events. EFTA01703108 does not substitute for EFTA00013764.
