What this video examines

This Short follows the initial exchange: Nili asks about meeting in New York, Epstein says he is in the Caribbean, and the discussion becomes a proposed St. Thomas visit.

The later email names the proposed inbound flight and asks whether the schedule suits Epstein. His reply approves the plan and assigns coordination; neither email alone proves travel occurred.

The completed visit is established by the separate office arrival and farewell thread linked in the written investigation. The clock time in the original video title is not treated here as a timezone-reconciled date finding.

What this does not prove

The completed December 12–15 visit is documented. Cohen = Barak is Thomas’s unproven hypothesis; a real associate traveling in parallel is the alternative. The return dates differ, and the records do not establish boarding under another identity, criminal conduct, or attendance by Summers or Pritzker.
