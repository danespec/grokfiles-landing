What this video examines

This Short focuses on the office’s question about the hotel booking name and Nili’s answer choosing her own name. The same correspondence also describes the security detail’s separate nearby accommodation.

That is direct evidence of a hotel reservation instruction. It is not direct evidence of concealment motive, an airline alias or the identity printed on a separate itinerary.

The surrounding arrival and farewell messages help distinguish a completed visit from the earlier planning emails. Read the thread as a sequence rather than treating the striking phrase as a conclusion.
