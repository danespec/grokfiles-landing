Reading rule

Testimony proves that a statement was made. It does not, by itself, prove the statement is true.

What the transcript establishes

The transcript establishes the sequence of the June 26, 2026 interview, the questions asked, Black's answers and refusals, counsel interventions, the Birthday Book exhibit discussion, subpoena service, counsel's closing statement, witness departure, and minority questions that were not reached.

What it does not establish

It does not independently prove Black's knowledge state, the truth of payment-service valuations, the full tax treatment, the full loan paper trail, the contents of undisclosed NDAs, or wrongdoing by any person named in Epstein's network.
