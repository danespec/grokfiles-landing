Executive finding

The transcript establishes what Doug Band said under committee questioning; it does not independently prove the truth of every recollection or denial.

What the transcript establishes

It establishes the June 30, 2026 interview sequence, Band's stated Clinton-office role, his account of limited Epstein contact, his Maxwell friendship account and denials, the EFTA exhibits staff used, his flight-log memory limits, his 2011 removal-list testimony, and his final third-party communication answers.

What the transcript does not establish

It does not by itself prove criminal participation, knowledge of trafficking, a complete travel record, the truth of every recollection, the validity of every committee premise, or wrongdoing by any person named in a flight log, email, address book, event, or question list.

What the transcript is

What Band personally claims

The core testimony is a set of qualified recollections and denials.

Band said his Epstein contact came through Clinton work, described Maxwell as a social friend, denied a romantic or sexual relationship with Maxwell, denied contemporaneous knowledge of improper conduct by Epstein or Maxwell, and said he tried to remove Maxwell from Clinton contact lists in 2011. Each of those points is published here as testimony unless a separate primary record supports the narrow point.
