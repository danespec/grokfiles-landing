Epstein Custody Records: 2019 Timeline

This is the administrative-record chronology surrounding detention and evidence preservation. The detailed August 9–10 incident sequence remains at the flagship final-48-hours timeline.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Dated source checkpoints

  1. 2019-06-27

    Record checkpoint: Morning-watch operations log, before Epstein’s July detention.

    Source and limit: EFTA00033413. Baseline document categories, not an Epstein-specific observation.

  2. 2019-07-22

    Record checkpoint: Morning-watch lieutenant operations log.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035570. Routine operations entries are not all inmate-specific.

  3. 2019-07-23

    Record checkpoint: Custody memorandum describing a reported incident.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035574. A reported incident is not a clinical diagnosis.

  4. 2019-08-08

    Record checkpoint: An attorney-visit log entry is reviewed in the published dossier.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035147. The row does not establish conversation contents.

  5. 2019-08-09 to 2019-08-10

    Record checkpoint: Camera-file list covers recorded windows; the OIG separately describes camera failures.

    Source and limit: EFTA00039416 and file-window analysis. The list cannot reconstruct unseen footage.

  6. 2019-08-10

    Record checkpoint: Discovery reported; internal draft and separate MCC release and questions.

    Source and limit: EFTA00039025, EFTA00035420, EFTA00035421, EFTA00035657. No unsupported minute-level discovery time is added.

  7. 2019-08-11

    Record checkpoint: Forwarded press-inquiry guidance.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035442. Internal guidance is not proof of an outbound response.

  8. 2019-08-12

    Record checkpoint: Preservation-and-production request forwarded.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035053. Request, collection and retention are separate steps.

  9. 2019-08-14

    Record checkpoint: BOP legal-hold notice.

    Source and limit: EFTA00035035. A hold notice is not an inventory of preserved material.

  10. 2023-06

    Record checkpoint: Inspector General report on custody, care and supervision.

    Source and limit: Official OIG report. Serious custody failures were found; the report did not establish evidence contradicting the suicide determination.

Undated and unresolved material

The rounds notice (EFTA00035206) specifies an interval without verifying posting date or compliance. The handwritten visitor log (EFTA00035119) is not a manually authenticated attendance roster. A diagram or exhibit pointer does not prove an event. See autopsy exhibit-list limits for a separate forensic source distinction.
