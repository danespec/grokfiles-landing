Epstein Files Disclosure Timeline: 2025–2026

A historical chronology through November 2, 2026, limited to the published sources cited below. It is not a current docket-status certification; consult the linked filings and later reporting for subsequent events.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Dated source checkpoints

  1. 2025-03-22

    Documented checkpoint: FBI workflow chronology records victim-name processing and further DOJ review direction.

    Source and limit: Part 13 source audit. The directive and final implementation are not fully verified. The 1,115-name list is not a client list.

  2. 2025-07

    Documented checkpoint: FBI–DOJ emails show requests and coordination on statements.

    Source and limit: July email chain. Exact proposed text is redacted.

  3. 2025-11-19

    Documented checkpoint: Epstein Files Transparency Act enacted as Public Law 119-38.

    Source and limit: Enacted statutory text. Statutory requirement, not proof of implementation.

  4. 2025-12-19

    Documented checkpoint: Section 2’s 30-day statutory release deadline.

    Source and limit: Deadline and exceptions. Not a certification that all covered records were released.

  5. 2026-01-30

    Documented checkpoint: DOJ announced production of nearly 3.5 million pages.

    Source and limit: DOJ release announcement. Agency-reported production, not a complete category-by-category audit.

  6. 2026-07-30

    Documented checkpoint: DOJ represented in ECF 25 that materials were delivered for in-camera review.

    Source and limit: July docket tracker. Filing does not establish judicial acceptance or public production.

  7. 2026-10-08

    Documented checkpoint: FBI Vault lists Part 13 of the review records.

    Source and limit: Vault release source path. Publication date differs from the 2025 workflow dates.

  8. 2026-10-28

    Documented checkpoint: D.C. Circuit denied DOJ’s stay request; dismissal issue referred to merits panel.

    Source and limit: Signed appellate order. Not a final merits ruling or immediate release order.

  9. 2026-11-02

    Documented checkpoint: Joint notice says DOJ review of tagged foreign-language records has begun.

    Source and limit: ECF 62–63 source audit. Government representation, not independently verified completion. November 27 was requested, not ordered by this notice.

Keep the procedural layers distinct

A statutory deadline, agency release, party representation, requested order, signed judicial order and actual production are six different checkpoints. The March and July review records predate the Act; thematic overlap alone does not establish that they belong to the certified administrative record in the later litigation.
