Epstein Financial Records: Dated Evidence Trail

Financial dates must be interpreted by document class: a transaction date, SAR filing date, later review and congressional statement are different types of evidence. These selected tracks do not form one continuous ledger.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Dated source checkpoints

  1. 2002–2016

    Financial record checkpoint: Seven JPMorgan SARs reported approximately $4.316 million in flagged activity.

    Source and limit: Nine-SAR reconstruction. Filing dates and underlying activity windows differ; flagged is not adjudicated criminal.

  2. 2012–2017

    Financial record checkpoint: Payment period covered by the later Dechert review of Leon Black’s payments.

    Source and limit: Payment-scope reconciliation. This is the review’s period, not a date for every payment.

  3. 2015-10-14

    Financial record checkpoint: Bank-derived exhibit records a $10 million BV70 LLC credit to Gratitude America’s MMDA.

    Source and limit: EFTA00080260 and account reconstruction. One tracked account, not charity-wide spending.

  4. 2016

    Financial record checkpoint: Reported $8.45 million same-charity transfers and $325,000 external payments from the tracked MMDA.

    Source and limit: EFTA00080260. Do not count transfers as grants or infer their subsequent uses.

  5. 2019-08 to 2019-09

    Financial record checkpoint: Two additional JPMorgan SAR filings covered about $1.283 billion of activity dating back years.

    Source and limit: Reporting-gap investigation. A reporting total is not an independent crime finding.

  6. 2020-03

    Financial record checkpoint: Financial-intelligence correspondence about a French inquiry and referenced SARs.

    Source and limit: EFTA00153569. Request and referenced reports are not findings of wrongdoing.

  7. 2021-01-22

    Financial record checkpoint: Commissioned Dechert review reported $158 million in Black payments.

    Source and limit: SEC-filed Dechert review. Outside counsel’s findings, not a judgment.

  8. 2021-04-26

    Financial record checkpoint: FBI import form references IRS forensic-accounting review.

    Source and limit: EFTA00128841. Full accountant report not included.

  9. 2023-06-16

    Financial record checkpoint: Forensic-accounting expert report cited in later Senate work.

    Source and limit: EFTA02810827 and report source brief. Expert analysis is not a court finding.

  10. 2025-03-12

    Financial record checkpoint: Senate Finance reported a $170 million payment total.

    Source and limit: 2025 Senate update and scope comparison. Do not add it to the $158 million total.

  11. 2025-11-19

    Financial record checkpoint: Senate Finance staff memorandum reproduces the nine-SAR chronology.

    Source and limit: Senate memorandum source path. Distinguish memorandum publication from the dates of the original reports.

How to reconcile the records

Account holder, transaction direction, activity window, reporting date and source type must match before figures can be compared. Receiving-account records, the actual referenced SAR narratives and the accountant’s complete report remain unresolved in the Gratitude America investigation. Separate bank and payment tracks are not evidence of a transfer between them.
