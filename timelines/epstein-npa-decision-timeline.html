Epstein NPA Timeline: July–December 2007

Read the 2007 decision chronology in two stages: the move toward a state resolution, followed by the dispute over notifying victims. The 2020 OPR review is a later reconstruction, not a contemporaneous decision memorandum.

Scope: Curated published-source navigation, not a comprehensive corpus review, newly authenticated evidence or a finding of wrongdoing. Dates, record classes and evidentiary limitations remain separate.

Dated source checkpoints

  1. 2007-07-19

    Documented checkpoint: Target-letter activity paused while management considered the case.

    Source and limit: July decision chain; EFTA00225044, p. 21. The statement was made in 2019; it is a later recollection.

  2. 2007-07-26

    Documented checkpoint: The investigative team was told of the proposed two-year state resolution.

    Source and limit: EFTA00225044, p. 22 and OPR record. The exact origin of the two-year term remains unresolved.

  3. 2007-07-31

    Documented checkpoint: Prosecutors circulated and presented the first documented non-prosecution term sheet.

    Source and limit: Term-sheet source chain. A term sheet is not the final signed agreement.

  4. 2007-09-06

    Documented checkpoint: The absence of victim consultation was raised in an email.

    Source and limit: Consultation chronology. Later recollections of the oral discussion differ.

  5. 2007-09-24

    Documented checkpoint: The NPA was signed before victims had been informed or consulted about the resolution.

    Source and limit: OPR reconstruction and NPA record. Distinguish the documented process from the later CVRA legal dispute.

  6. 2007-11-30

    Documented checkpoint: The office stated a December 7 notification deadline.

    Source and limit: November 30 deadline source. The later hold was not explained by this deadline alone.

  7. 2007-12-06

    Documented checkpoint: An FBI agent warned of earlier notification gaps.

    Source and limit: December 6 warning and defense papers. Not a complete decision record.

  8. 2007-12-07

    Documented checkpoint: 17:05:56 EST request to send; 17:08:19 EST instruction to hold.

    Source and limit: EFTA00013764 and native-email analysis. The 2-minute-23-second interval does not identify the consulted person.

  9. 2020-11-12

    Documented checkpoint: DOJ announced OPR’s findings: poor judgment, without a finding of professional misconduct.

    Source and limit: DOJ public statement. This is a later review, not a contemporaneous cause of the 2007 decisions.

What remains unresolved

The dated sequence supports a documented change in course and a later notification hold. It does not establish the origin of the exact plea term, the identity of the person consulted before the hold, or an intelligence explanation. The later federal prosecution belongs to a separate case.
