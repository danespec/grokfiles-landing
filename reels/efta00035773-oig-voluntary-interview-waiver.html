EFTA00035773: the OIG interview waiver and its date discrepancy

Read EFTA00035773 with GAH’s original caption, voluntary-interview terms, redactions and the unresolved August 21 versus August 27 date reading.

GrokArchiveHub · Source notes published October 26, 2026

Read the document behind the reel

The form explains that an employee is being asked to provide information voluntarily and may decline without disciplinary action for that choice. It also states that a furnished statement may be used in later criminal or disciplinary proceedings. Those are the form’s terms, not a finding about an employee’s conduct.

The original reel calls the date August 21. Visual inspection of the mirrored page suggests the handwritten field reads 8/27/19. That discrepancy is visible here and remains subject to confirmation against the original release or matching interview record.

The handwritten subject concerns Epstein and employee misconduct at the MCC. The redacted signature fields do not identify an interviewee, and a signed waiver does not publish their answers or establish that misconduct occurred. This one form is not proof of a universal staff-interview practice.

What this does not prove

This is a warning-and-waiver form, not an interview transcript, a finding of misconduct or proof that every staff member signed the same form. It records voluntary participation terms and the possible evidentiary use of a statement. The handwritten date appears to read August 27, 2019; the caption’s August 21 is not adopted as a verified date. Redactions prevent identification of the employee, and the form alone does not establish what was said or whether an interview was completed. Source access: the reviewed PDF is a Roll Call-hosted mirror of the released record. Its visible Bates footer was checked against the filename; no byte-for-byte comparison with a fresh DOJ download is claimed. The DOJ URL returned age verification. Source portraits, signatures, private contact details and neighboring inmates’ identities are not republished.
