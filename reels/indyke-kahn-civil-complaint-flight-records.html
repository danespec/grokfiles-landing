Read the document behind the reel

The civil complaint and the two older records perform different jobs: the complaint states the State’s allegations, the FAA paperwork records a corporate role, and the email records travel coordination. None automatically substantiates the others’ criminal significance.

The original caption and the defendants’ reported denial stay together. This page does not turn names missing from a reviewed passenger log into proof that they never traveled, or turn a conditional bequest into a completed estate payment.

Document receipts and their limits

  1. EFTA00016622 — official DOJ PDF

    Secondary mirror of EFTA00016622 (access copy; not byte-verified against DOJ).

    FAA aircraft paperwork identifies Shmitka Air as owner and Darren Indyke as signer with the typed title Vice President. A corporate role is not proof of passenger travel, knowledge of abuse or criminal participation. Linked as a publisher-hosted EFTA-stamped released PDF mirror, not byte-authenticated against DOJ.

  2. EFTA02213201 — official DOJ PDF

    Secondary mirror of EFTA02213201 (access copy; not byte-verified against DOJ).

    May 2017 Groff/Kahn chain concerns proposed one-way JFK-to-St. Thomas travel for Michael and Kimberly, including carrier information and coordination. It is not a Little St. James arrival record or a log of Kahn’s own travel.

  3. NMDOJ filed civil complaint, October 21, 2026 — official NMDOJ complaint PDF

    October 21, 2026 civil complaint supplied by NMDOJ, 49 pages, case 1:26-cv-03543. It alleges knowing facilitation and seeks relief. It describes possible 50 million and 25 million trust bequests subject to available funds, not cash already paid.

Leak IDs identify a separate mailbox source family. They are labeled rather than assigned invented public document URLs. An archive locator is not itself authentication of every statement in a document.

What this does not prove

The State’s allegations are not findings or a criminal conviction. Indyke and Kahn, through counsel, deny knowingly assisting Epstein’s abuse and dispute the suit, as carried in the existing investigation. The reel’s “They weren’t passengers” goes beyond an absence in reviewed logs and is not adopted as a universal travel finding. The travel email concerns St. Thomas, not independently proven island arrival. Potential trust bequests are conditional, not paid distributions.

Open questions and records still needed

  • Underlying flight, carrier and payment records for each actual movement.
  • Docket responses and orders, original trust provisions and estate accounting before treating requested relief or possible bequests as completed outcomes.
