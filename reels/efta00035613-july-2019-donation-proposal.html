EFTA00035613: the five-million-dollar proposal in a July 2019 letter

Read the two-page EFTA00035613 letter with GAH’s original MCC reel caption, the proposed gift, institutional limits and source provenance.

GrokArchiveHub · Source notes published October 26, 2026

Read the document behind the reel

The July 30 letter proposes financial support as a way to improve Epstein’s public position. The two-page document supplies the proposal and the author’s assertions; it does not supply a university decision, donation receipt or response from Epstein.

The letter is signed Jonathan Farley and lists an Oxford doctorate. It describes Oxford as his alma mater and presents a possible future lectureship as conditional. The original caption’s Oxford mathematician shorthand is therefore qualified rather than presented as proof he spoke for Oxford.

Morgan State is a proposed recipient in the author’s account, not a verified institutional participant in an accepted arrangement. The letter’s references to conversations and reputation benefits remain attributed to its author. No contact details or portraits from the source pages are reproduced.

What this does not prove

The letter does not establish that Epstein replied, paid, accepted the proposal or received the letter. It does not establish Morgan State University or Oxford approved an arrangement. The author describes Oxford as his alma mater and a future lectureship as hypothetical; the caption’s Oxford shorthand is not proof of a current Oxford appointment. His descriptions of institutional conversations remain his statements. Source access: the reviewed PDF is a Roll Call-hosted mirror of the released record. Its visible Bates footer was checked against the filename; no byte-for-byte comparison with a fresh DOJ download is claimed. The DOJ URL returned age verification. Source portraits, signatures, private contact details and neighboring inmates’ identities are not republished.
