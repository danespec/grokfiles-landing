Read the document behind the reel

A donation solicitation uses Zuckerberg’s awards as a model; an unrelated Davos promotional email uses his name to advertise networking. In both, someone else names him.

Neither released record shows Zuckerberg writing to Epstein, agreeing to a meeting or attending an event with him. The caption’s wider negative-search claim remains attributed to its original scope rather than promoted into proof that no contact ever occurred.

Document receipts and their limits

  1. DOJ EFTA00034221

    July 2019 donation solicitation to Epstein invokes Zuckerberg’s Breakthrough Awards as a comparison. That is the writer’s pitch, not correspondence from Zuckerberg or independent verification of its claims.

  2. DOJ EFTA02262778

    November 2018 forwarded Davos accommodation/event promotion names Zuckerberg among people attendees might meet. Promotional copy is not an attendance record or evidence of contact with Epstein.

Leak IDs identify a separate mailbox source family. They are labeled rather than assigned invented public document URLs. An archive locator is not itself authentication of every statement in a document.

What this does not prove

These two records are third-party mentions, not evidence of direct communication. The original caption’s “Contact: zero” is a negative-search finding attributed to GAH’s stated research scope. This page does not independently certify an exhaustive search of every corpus, prove that contact never occurred, or establish wrongdoing.

Open questions and records still needed

  • A direct communication or authenticated meeting record would be different evidence from a third-party mention.
  • A reproducible search log is needed to independently audit the caption’s corpus-wide negative finding.
