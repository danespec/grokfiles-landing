What this does not prove

These are financial-office and maintenance communications, not findings of criminal conduct. The redacted payee remains unidentified. The caption’s characterization of an apartment provided by Epstein and its one-word follow-up account go beyond what the reviewed EFTA00962798 page independently establishes; they are not adopted as new findings here.
