The reel introduces the existing five-track audit. The strongest distinction is between a transfer anticipated in correspondence and a completed payment independently matched at both banks.

A million dollars was meant for a 'Clinton project.' The record can't prove it moved. Our source audit of the Clinton Foundation, CGI and Epstein tracks five evidence lines: a prospective $1M 2004 Publicis transfer (no matched debit, credit, invoice, or payer), a reported $25K 2006 grant, TerraMar's 2013 CGI participation, and testimony on CGI's origins. Leak IDs identify a separate mailbox source family. They are labeled rather than assigned invented public document URLs. An archive locator is not itself authentication of every statement in a document.

What this does not prove

No matched debit, beneficiary credit, invoice or ultimate payer establishes that the proposed 2004 transfer completed. A donor tax-return entry is not a cleared recipient deposit. A CGI commitment is not necessarily Foundation revenue. No wrongdoing or causal explanation of later Foundation revenue changes follows from these sources. Later TerraMar and witness accounts belong to the separate source tracks in the existing audit.

Open questions and records still needed

  • The originating debit, beneficiary credit and invoice for the proposed Publicis transfer.
  • Recipient acknowledgement of the reported grant, and contemporaneous CGI formation and TerraMar commitment records.
