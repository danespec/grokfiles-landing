EFTA02300238 — Hoffman’s November 2014 Travel Planning

Document context

A November 28, 2014 email from Lesley Groff to Reid Hoffman and Joi Ito forwards pilot information for a proposed November 30 island-to-Bedford trip. It requests home addresses for customs pre-clearance; those private details are not reproduced.

What the record supports

The correspondence describes arrangements and expected timing. It is not a passenger manifest or an observed arrival record. Earlier template text incorrectly grouped this identifier with the separate December Barak trip.

Limits and open questions

The email does not prove boarding, operation of the flight or criminal conduct. Customs clearance and passenger-level confirmation require separate records.
