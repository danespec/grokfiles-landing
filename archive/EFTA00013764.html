What the email establishes

GAH's original-source analysis reports that Assistant U.S. Attorney A. Marie Villafaña asked at 17:05:56 whether the victim-notification letter could be sent. The reply email header records 22:08:19 UTC, equivalent to 17:08:19 Eastern Standard Time, and its text instructs, “Hold the letter.” That is a documented 2-minute-23-second interval within the correspondence—not proof of what external conversations occurred during it.

Evidentiary limits

The document does not identify any unnamed person whom Jeffrey Sloman may have consulted before sending the instruction. It does not establish that Main Justice ordered the hold, that intelligence considerations drove the non-prosecution agreement, or that a particular defense communication caused the hold. Read the full source reconstruction and the victim-notification analysis before making a causal claim.
