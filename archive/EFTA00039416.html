Competing interpretation and counterevidence

Video-continuity claims require video files, playback logs, camera maps, export logs, metadata, or certified chain-of-custody records. A file list can support a request for those materials but cannot replace them.

Why this record matters

The record is useful because it gives readers exact file-window language and directs them to the missing materials that would be needed for stronger video claims. It prevents the archive from turning a file list into a footage conclusion.
