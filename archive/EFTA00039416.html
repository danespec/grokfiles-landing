Limits and open questions

What the record does not establish

  • It does not show the video contents.
  • It does not prove continuous camera coverage or the absence of gaps.
  • It does not identify every camera angle, blind spot, file omission, or chain-of-custody step.
  • It does not establish motive, intent, conduct, or cause of death.

Competing interpretation and counterevidence

Video-continuity claims require video files, playback logs, camera maps, export logs, metadata, or certified chain-of-custody records. A file list can support a request for those materials but cannot replace them.

Redaction, OCR, metadata, and authenticity limits

Readers should inspect the rendered pages because file names and timestamps can be misread by OCR. The dossier does not assert that the listed file windows are complete or that the source pages show all available video evidence.

Open receipt slots

  • Actual CH0080 video files or certified excerpts.
  • Camera map and blind-spot documentation.
  • Export logs, metadata, chain-of-custody records, and playback notes.
  • Clarification of whether the file list is complete for the relevant period.

Reading limits

Presence ≠ conduct; chronology ≠ causation; open slots ≠ findings.
