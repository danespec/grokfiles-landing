Limits and open questions

What the record does not establish

  • It does not replace the full autopsy report, court records, EMS records, hospital records, raw video, BOP internal logs, or every underlying interview.
  • It does not make missing records disappear; missing or unavailable records remain open receipt slots.
  • It does not convert official silence into proof of an alternate theory.

Competing interpretation and counterevidence

The archive treats the OIG report as a strong official source, not as the only permissible record. Competing interpretations must point to durable records: court filings, medical examiner materials, BOP records, video logs, EMS records, hospital records, or corrected official releases.

Redaction, OCR, metadata, and authenticity limits

The official PDF is the reader-facing source. The archive hash verifies the local artifact state, but the dossier does not claim custody over every upstream source used by OIG. OCR should be treated as a finding aid unless checked against the PDF text or page image.

Open receipt slots
