Limits and open questions

What this record cannot establish

The passage is prosecutors’ summary of Wexner’s lawyers’ account, not sworn testimony, underlying bank records, the executed power of attorney or an adjudicated accounting. Counsel denied Wexner knew about Epstein’s inappropriate or unlawful activity with young women and characterized the relationship as more professional than social; those are attributed positions, not findings adopted here. The misappropriation and wealth explanations likewise remain attributed. The reel’s claims that the power of attorney is absent across the releases and that there is no Wexner-authored document are historical scoped search findings, not independently re-run corpus-wide conclusions. This page does not establish wrongdoing by Wexner.
