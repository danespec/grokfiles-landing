What This Is

A controlled hypothesis workspace.

The Book of Black is a compiled research manuscript that can contain candidate relationships, aliases, logistical patterns, timeline questions, unresolved theories, search leads, potential contradictions, and hypotheses requiring independent verification.

Its role is to create testable questions. The archive decides what survives.

What This Is Not

It is not the evidence layer.

  • Not primary evidence.
  • Not verified intelligence.
  • Not a finding of guilt.
  • Not proof of identity or association.
  • Not authority for identifying redacted people.
  • Not a substitute for the archive.
  • Not a finished historical account.

How To Use It

Every manuscript lead must survive a source test.

  1. Book of Black hypothesisStart with the claim as written.
  2. Archive searchLook for records outside the manuscript.
  3. Source discoveryOpen the original record where possible.
  4. Provenance reviewTrack source type, custody, OCR, and context.
  5. Bias and silence analysisRecord what the source can and cannot show.
  6. Corroboration or contradictionLet the evidence change the status.

Why Skepticism Matters

Contradiction is a result.

Skepticism should apply to official narratives. It should also apply to alternative theories. A hypothesis earns credibility only by surviving contact with evidence. Failed theories should remain visible rather than being quietly deleted, because a retired lead can teach the next reader where the record stopped.
