Verified Epstein Entity Index

A source-first directory for people, companies, banks, partnerships, government bodies, and institutions appearing in Grok Archive Hub’s Epstein research. The index records documentary relationships without turning presence into accusation.

Search the archive Banking Records Investigations Methodology

Identity-control rule: inclusion means the entity is supported by a published GAH source lane or primary-source chain. It does not imply guilt, knowledge, agency, conspiracy, liability, participation in another person’s conduct, or criminal purpose.

Relationship labels are documentary descriptions, not conclusions about motive. “Paid,” “banked,” “introduced,” “appears in,” “counterparty,” and “reviewed” are used only where the cited source lane supports those narrower verbs.

How to use the index

Entity type

Person, company, bank, partnership, government body, institution, or expert role.

Source lane

The part of the documentary record where the entity is currently verified.

Verified relationship

The narrowest relationship the published sources support.

Boundary

The strongest inference the source does not support without additional receipts.

Machine-readable index

The same control layer is published as entities.json. Each entry contains the entity type, source lane, verified relationship, GAH route, source classes, and evidentiary boundary.

Promotion rule

GAH will not promote a person or organization into this index solely because a name appears in an address book, flight log, calendar, photograph, contact list, or unverified allegation. A row requires a source-controlled lane that lets the archive state a narrow, verifiable relationship without filling gaps.
